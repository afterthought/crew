# bolt-teams Specification

## Purpose
A bolt team builds one bolt at a time and holds at most one more that waits to land: a standing conductor and ops, and units built stage by stage by fresh agents. The bolt is a branch that moves only through sluice, and lands on main once its verification is green.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Teams are fixed in the teams file
crew SHALL read its teams from `~/.config/crew/teams.json` version 2 or version 3, which the machine's configuration writes and crew never edits. Each team names its system, machine, session, its number of unit slots (`units`), and its repos: its kits, one or more, then its partition's blueprints repo, each named once. In version 3 each team SHALL also name `flume`: `false` while it runs today's path, or the name of the Flume stream its work runs in on its host. A version-2 file SHALL be read with every team on today's path, since its schema holds no setting. A team's partition SHALL be the partition of its session in `herdr-hosts.json`. A team with data missing or inconsistent SHALL be an error, never a default.

#### Scenario: A blueprints repo of another partition
- **WHEN** a team in a `clients/willdan` session names afterthought's blueprints as its last repo
- **THEN** crew refuses to load the team, naming the partition's blueprints repos

#### Scenario: The first version of the file
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 1
- **THEN** crew says the file must be regenerated by the machine's configuration, naming versions 2 and 3, and does nothing

#### Scenario: Two teams hold one kit
- **WHEN** slc-1's repos are sluice, weir and blueprints, and flm-1's are flume, weir and blueprints
- **THEN** both load, each holding weir beside its own kit, with no team of weir's own

#### Scenario: Version 3 names each team's setting
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 3, crw-4's `flume` is `"madswan"` and crw-5's is `false`
- **THEN** crew loads crw-4 with its work's stream `madswan` and crw-5 on today's path

#### Scenario: A team in version 3 without its setting
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 3 and crw-6 names no `flume`, or names it `true`
- **THEN** crew refuses to load the file, naming crw-6, the file, and that `flume` is `false` or the name of a stream

#### Scenario: Version 2 still loads
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 2, as swancloud writes it before its version-3 deploy
- **THEN** every team loads on today's path

### Requirement: A team's standing roles are its conductor and ops
`crew up <team>` SHALL start the conductor and ops, and nothing else. The conductor keeps the bolt's units moving and tells the user where they stand. Ops does everything that touches a live system for the bolt, including its deploys and tests.

#### Scenario: A team comes up
- **WHEN** `crew up swb-1` runs
- **THEN** `swb-1-conductor` and `swb-1-ops` start, and no other agent of the team is running

### Requirement: Units run in slots
A team SHALL have at most `units` units in flight, each held by a slot named `<team>-unit-<n>` with its own pane. A slot SHALL be taken when a unit's first stage starts, and freed when the unit merges into the bolt or is dropped. A slot whose unit or fix has merged into its bolt or landed on main, or whose work the plan no longer has (a dropped unit, or a fix whose bolt was dropped), SHALL be freed the next time crew reads the team (`crew status`, `crew unit run`, `crew fix`), once the slot's agent is not working. A fix SHALL read landed once the kit's main holds its branch's tip and the branch has moved since it was made, or, for one that never moved, once its bolt is in no plan and main holds its tip; a fix SHALL be read from its branch whether or not its place still stands.

#### Scenario: Every slot is taken
- **WHEN** the conductor starts a fifth unit on a team with `units = 4`, all four in flight
- **THEN** crew refuses, naming the units the slots hold

#### Scenario: A unit dropped while its stage works
- **WHEN** the planner drops a unit while its slot's agent is working, and that agent later settles
- **THEN** the next `crew status` of the team frees the slot, and the unit's worktree and branch go as for any work that is over

#### Scenario: A fix whose bolt has landed
- **WHEN** a slot holds a fix that merged into its bolt, the bolt has landed, and the kit's main holds the fix's tip
- **THEN** the next `crew status` of the team reads the fix landed and frees the slot once its agent is not working

#### Scenario: A fix whose place is gone
- **WHEN** a slot holds a fix whose place was removed while its branch stands, and the fix has merged into its bolt or landed with it
- **THEN** the next `crew status` of the team reads it merged or landed from its branch and frees the slot, as it would with its place standing

#### Scenario: A fix just made is not landed
- **WHEN** a fix's branch was cut from its bolt at a commit main already holds, has no commit of its own, and its bolt is still planned
- **THEN** the fix reads in flight, and its slot is kept

### Requirement: A unit is built stage by stage, each by a fresh agent
`crew unit run <unit> <stage>` SHALL start the stage in the unit's slot with a new agent, after ending the one there. The new agent gets that stage's brief and effort, starts in the unit's worktree, and is sent its command. The stages are:

- construct: `/opsx:propose` with the unit's intent and sources;
- code: `/opsx:apply`;
- verify: `/opsx:verify`;
- settle: the coder's brief, with the forecast or amendment that reached a unit waiting to merge;
- merge: a submission to the bolt made without waiting, `sluice submit bolt/<bolt> --no-wait --json`, which its agent waits on a call at a time with `sluice wait <n> --timeout 9m --json` until it exits other than 5, still waiting, and which sluice lands only when the checks the kit's policy requires of its bolt branches pass on the bolt with the unit merged.

Code SHALL start as soon as the unit's change is committed, with no review between.

#### Scenario: Construct
- **WHEN** `crew unit run a-tenant-creates-its-environments construct` runs
- **THEN** a fresh agent in `places/a-tenant-creates-its-environments` writes the unit's OpenSpec change from its intent and sources, commits it, and stops

#### Scenario: Code before review
- **WHEN** `crew unit run <unit> code` runs on a unit whose stage is `planned`, whose change no batch has yet held
- **THEN** a fresh code agent starts, and nobody was asked to approve the change

#### Scenario: Code before construct has committed
- **WHEN** `crew unit run <unit> code` runs on a unit in `construct`
- **THEN** it is refused until its change is committed

#### Scenario: Verify before the tasks are done
- **WHEN** `crew unit run <unit> verify` runs on a unit in `code`
- **THEN** it is refused, naming the tasks still open

#### Scenario: Merging
- **WHEN** a verified unit's merge stage runs
- **THEN** its agent is told to run `sluice submit bolt/<bolt> --no-wait --json` in the unit's place and to wait on the submission with `sluice wait`, and sluice's verdict decides whether the unit lands on the bolt

#### Scenario: A slow gate
- **WHEN** a unit's merge has been testing for twenty minutes
- **THEN** its agent's calls of `sluice wait <n> --timeout 9m --json` have each exited 5 within ten minutes, and its next call ends with sluice's verdict, exiting 0 when the unit landed

### Requirement: Work happens in fixed places
A bolt SHALL be the branch `bolt/<bolt>`, made on GitHub from main by sluice at crew's give, with no worktree, and SHALL move only through sluice. A unit SHALL be built on `unit/<unit>`, branched from `origin/bolt/<bolt>` after a fetch, tracking it, and holding its `After` units' work as it stood when its place was made, in `<kit>/places/<unit>`; a bolt made on its host before gives went through sluice SHALL have its places branched from the local `bolt/<bolt>`, tracking it, until it lands. A chore's and a fix's place SHALL be cut the same way. When a branch of the unit's name was kept by an earlier drop, the unit's place SHALL be made on that branch as it stands, the branch SHALL then track the unit's bolt where it lives, `origin/bolt/<bolt>` for a bolt on GitHub and the local `bolt/<bolt>` for one made before, and the stage that made the place SHALL say it starts from the kept branch, naming the commit it is at; a chore's place SHALL be made on a kept `chore/<chore>` alike. A unit SHALL merge into its bolt only by a submission through sluice. Its place SHALL be removed once it has merged, as every worktree crew made is once its work is over. Each worktree crew makes SHALL be made ready through its kit's declaration before any agent starts in it, and by no script a kit names for crew. A team's conductor and ops SHALL start in the kit's main checkout, a detached anchor at `origin/main` that the fleet's timer moves and nobody edits in, and SHALL read the bolt by ref. No worktree crew makes or starts an agent in SHALL be on `main` or on a bolt branch, except the landing's short-lived worktree.

#### Scenario: A bolt is given
- **WHEN** `crew bolt give swb-1` gives swb-1 the bolt `b`
- **THEN** `bolt/b` is made on GitHub from main by sluice, no worktree is made for it, and swb-1's conductor and ops start in the kit's main checkout, which is detached

#### Scenario: A unit merges
- **WHEN** the merge stage of a verified unit submits it and sluice lands it
- **THEN** `bolt/<bolt>` holds the unit's change in a merge commit sluice made, `places/<unit>` is gone, and the unit's slot is free

#### Scenario: A place is cut from the bolt on GitHub
- **WHEN** crew makes a unit's, chore's or fix's place on a host whose local `bolt/<bolt>` lags the branch on GitHub
- **THEN** the place is cut from `origin/bolt/<bolt>` after a fetch, tracks it, and holds every merge sluice landed there

#### Scenario: A place of a bolt made before
- **WHEN** crew makes a unit's place for a bolt whose branch is on its host only, its tip's policy naming no remote for it
- **THEN** the place is cut from the local `bolt/<bolt>`, tracks it, and nothing is fetched or made on GitHub for it

#### Scenario: The bolt on GitHub cannot be fetched
- **WHEN** crew makes a place for a bolt on GitHub and the fetch fails or GitHub has no `bolt/<bolt>`
- **THEN** no place is made, and crew names the bolt and the fetch's words

#### Scenario: A kit with a crew-prepare script
- **WHEN** crew makes a unit's place in a kit whose main checkout's devenv profile holds a `crew-prepare` script
- **THEN** the script does not run

#### Scenario: A unit added again under a kept branch's name
- **WHEN** `unit/u` was kept when unit `u` was dropped from bolt `a`, and a unit `u` added later to bolt `b` starts construct
- **THEN** `places/u` is made on `unit/u` as it stood, holding the kept commits, `unit/u` tracks bolt `b` where it lives, `origin/bolt/b` for a bolt on GitHub, and the construct's start says the place starts from the kept `unit/u` at its commit

### Requirement: Fixes during a bolt are built beside its units
`crew fix <team> <name> "<what is wrong>"` SHALL make `fix/<bolt>/<name>` from the team's bolt at `<kit>/places/fix-<bolt>--<name>`. While the team holds two bolts, a new fix SHALL name its bolt with `--bolt <bolt>`, one the team holds, and SHALL be refused without it, naming both; a fix in flight SHALL be found by its name among the team's bolts, and a name in flight on both SHALL be refused without `--bolt`. It SHALL start a fresh code agent there in a free slot with the words given, whether or not the place could be made ready: when a step of its readying failed, the agent SHALL be told the step and its last lines with the fix's words, since a fix is how a bolt is repaired, and the command SHALL say so. A new fix whose branch or place already exists SHALL be refused, naming the place, and a fix's agent SHALL start only in a worktree on that fix's own branch, made from the fix's bolt. `crew fix <team> <name> --merge` SHALL start its merge stage, and the fix SHALL merge into the bolt like a unit, by a submission. `crew fix <team> <name> --drop` SHALL end a fix that is not wanted, whether a slot holds it or only its branch or place stands: its slot SHALL be freed and its agent ended when one is up, a working agent only with `--force`, and its place and branch SHALL go as a dropped unit's do, its branch kept and named when it holds commits whose patch the bolt lacks. A fix that has merged into its bolt SHALL NOT be dropped, and a drop refused SHALL be recorded. A fix SHALL have no OpenSpec change and no plan record. A landing that conflicts with main SHALL be taken by a fix whose agent merges main into its place, resolving the conflict, and which merges into the bolt like any fix; nothing of crew's SHALL move the bolt's branch.

#### Scenario: A red suite on the bolt
- **WHEN** a unit's or fix's submission to the bolt fails a check the kit's policy requires
- **THEN** nothing lands, the bolt is where it was, and the agent fixes the cause in its own place and submits again

#### Scenario: Two teams name a fix the same
- **WHEN** two teams that build in one kit checkout each start a fix named `bolt-takes-main` on their own bolts
- **THEN** each fix has its own branch made from its own team's bolt, `fix/<bolt>/bolt-takes-main`, in its own place, `places/fix-<bolt>--bolt-takes-main`, and each team's status shows only its own fix

#### Scenario: A fix on the bolt that waits to land
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows`, proven, and `ssh-fix`, and the conductor runs `crew fix swc-1 take-main "Take main into the bolt: README.md" --bolt doctor-rows`
- **THEN** the fix is made on `fix/doctor-rows/take-main` from `bolt/doctor-rows` in a free slot, and `crew fix swc-1 take-main --merge` submits it to `bolt/doctor-rows`

#### Scenario: A new fix that names no bolt while two are held
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows` and `ssh-fix` and the conductor runs `crew fix swc-1 gap "<words>"`
- **THEN** crew refuses, naming both bolts and `--bolt`, takes no slot and starts no agent

#### Scenario: A fix name already in use on the bolt
- **WHEN** a team starts a fix whose branch or place already exists, and none of its slots holds that fix
- **THEN** crew refuses it, naming the place, takes no slot and starts no agent, and records the refusal

#### Scenario: A fix in flight is run again
- **WHEN** a team starts a fix that one of its slots already holds
- **THEN** the fix's agent starts again in that fix's own place, on its own branch

#### Scenario: A place that is not the fix's own
- **WHEN** a fix's place is on a branch other than the fix's own, or its branch was made from another bolt than the fix's
- **THEN** crew refuses to start the fix's agent there, naming the place and the branch or bolt it is on

#### Scenario: A fix that adds commits merges
- **WHEN** a fix's branch holds a commit of its own beyond the bolt, and the conductor runs `crew fix <team> <name> --merge`
- **THEN** crew starts the fix's merge stage, as for a unit, and its agent submits it to the bolt

#### Scenario: A fix takes main into the bolt
- **WHEN** a bolt's landing conflicted with main on `README.md`, a fix's agent merged main into the fix's place resolving it, and the conductor runs `crew fix <team> <name> --merge`
- **THEN** crew starts the fix's merge stage, its agent submits it to the bolt, and once sluice lands it the bolt holds main and the landing can be submitted again

#### Scenario: A bolt whose toolchain fails
- **WHEN** a team starts a fix and the bolt's declared toolchain or its runner's setup fails in the fix's new place
- **THEN** the fix's agent starts there, its prompt holding the fix's words, the step that failed and its last lines, and the start is recorded with no refusal

#### Scenario: A fix not wanted is dropped
- **WHEN** the conductor runs `crew fix <team> <name> --drop` on a fix a slot holds, whose agent is idle and whose branch holds nothing beyond the bolt
- **THEN** its slot is freed and its agent ended, its place and branch are removed through the kit's `wt remove`, and the drop is recorded

#### Scenario: A fix whose agent is working
- **WHEN** the conductor drops a fix whose agent is working, without `--force`
- **THEN** crew refuses, saying the agent is working and that `--force` ends it anyway, records the refusal, and frees nothing

#### Scenario: A fix standing with no slot
- **WHEN** a fix's branch and place stand with no slot holding them, the branch holding a commit of its own, and the conductor drops it
- **THEN** its place is removed, and its branch is kept, named with the commit it is at and how many commits of its own it holds

#### Scenario: A merged fix
- **WHEN** the conductor drops a fix that has merged into its bolt
- **THEN** crew refuses, saying it has merged and that a new fix undoes it, and records the refusal

### Requirement: A bolt lands on main on the user's word
Bolts whose verification is green SHALL be landed by the partition's main-level ops on the user's word, by one procedure: `crew bolt submit <bolt>...` submits each bolt's branch to its kit's main through sluice, in the order given, and each bolt that lands is closed with `crew bolt land`'s own code by the first crew act that reads it after sluice's record has it landed, `crew bolt submit`'s own end among them; it deploys nothing, pushes nothing and levels no host's checkout. Each SHALL be submitted from a short-lived worktree on its branch at `<kit>/places/land-<bolt>` on its team's host, in the name of whoever asked, removed once its outcome is in. A bolt that does not land SHALL move nothing, and the bolts after it SHALL go on. The command SHALL exit non-zero when any bolt did not land, naming each.

#### Scenario: The bolt lands
- **WHEN** the user sends swb-1's bolt ticked on its kit's landings card and main-level ops runs `crew bolt submit <bolt>`
- **THEN** sluice lands the bolt on GitHub's main as a merge commit, the landing's worktree is gone, the bolt is closed as `crew bolt land` closes it, the bolt's records are gone from the plan, and swb-1 can be given its next bolt

#### Scenario: Three bolts queued as one chain
- **WHEN** main-level ops runs `crew bolt submit y z w`, the three held by teams on one host
- **THEN** `y` is submitted first, `z` once sluice has queued `y`, and `w` once it has queued `z`, none waiting for the one before to land, so sluice tests them as one chain and lands them in that order

#### Scenario: Bolts on two hosts
- **WHEN** main-level ops runs `crew bolt submit y z`, `y` held by a team on deneb and `z` by one on mac-studio
- **THEN** `z` is submitted only once `y`'s submission has ended, so `z` never lands before `y`

#### Scenario: One refused, the rest go on
- **WHEN** `crew bolt submit y z w` runs and sluice refuses `z` for a conflict with main
- **THEN** `y` and `w` land and are closed, `z` and main are where they were for `z`, the command says `z` did not land in sluice's words and that its team takes main into it by a fix, and it exits non-zero

#### Scenario: Bolts of two kits
- **WHEN** `crew bolt submit y w` names a bolt of crew and a bolt of swancloud
- **THEN** it is refused before anything is submitted, saying it lands one kit's bolts at a time

#### Scenario: The waiting bolt lands while the team builds another
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows`, proven, and builds `ssh-fix`, and `crew bolt submit doctor-rows` lands it
- **THEN** swc-1 holds `ssh-fix` alone, the command says swc-1 builds `ssh-fix`, and the conductor's and ops's restart is not made due by the landing, since the branch they carry is `bolt/ssh-fix`

#### Scenario: The landing's rebase conflicts
- **WHEN** the bolt and main changed the same lines and `crew bolt submit` runs
- **THEN** the command says the bolt did not land with sluice's words, main and the bolt's branch are where they were, the landing's worktree is gone, and the conductor is to take main into the bolt by a fix

#### Scenario: Main is behind GitHub's
- **WHEN** `crew bolt submit` runs while the kit's main on the team's host lacks a commit GitHub's main has
- **THEN** nothing is refused for it, since sluice forms each landing on GitHub's main and pushes it with a lease

#### Scenario: A landed bolt crew could not close
- **WHEN** sluice lands bolt `y` and its close then refuses, its plan unreadable
- **THEN** the command says `y` landed and was not closed, naming why and that crew's next read of the bolt closes it once that is mended, the run record holds a refused `bolt.land` on `bolt/y` with those words, and the command goes on to the next bolt

#### Scenario: The bolt's worktree has uncommitted changes
- **WHEN** `crew bolt land` runs on a bolt given before bolts were branches, whose worktree has a modified file
- **THEN** the bolt lands and the team no longer holds it, and crew says the worktree is kept with its branch because it has uncommitted changes

### Requirement: A team's next bolt needs no edit and no deploy
Giving a team its next bolt SHALL be `crew bolt give <team>`, once every bolt it holds is proven and it holds fewer than two. A team's base branch SHALL come from the bolt it builds, never from the teams file.

#### Scenario: swb-1 finishes a bolt
- **WHEN** swb-1's bolt has landed and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give swb-1`
- **THEN** swb-1 holds the next planned bolt in switchboard-kit, its bolt branch exists, and nothing was deployed

#### Scenario: swb-1's bolt is proven and waits to land
- **WHEN** every unit of swb-1's bolt has merged, sluice is green at its head, and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give swb-1`
- **THEN** swb-1 holds the next planned bolt in switchboard-kit beside the one waiting to land, and nothing was deployed

### Requirement: A team's commands run on its host
A team's panes and state SHALL live on the team's host. A command about a team run on another host SHALL be run on the team's host, over ssh, by the crew checkout that host's sessions keep.

#### Scenario: Status from a Mac
- **WHEN** `crew status swb-1` runs on mac-studio
- **THEN** it reads swb-1's panes and slots on the box, and shows them

### Requirement: A team is two herdr workspaces
A team SHALL have two herdr workspaces in its session:

- `<team>`, with a `conductor` tab holding the conductor and ops, and a `git` tab with gitgui in the kit and the blueprints repo;
- `<team> units`, which exists only while a unit or fix is in flight, with one pane per slot laid out as tiles.

A session SHALL hold any number of teams.

#### Scenario: Two teams in one session
- **WHEN** swb-1 and swb-2 both run in `wldn-1`
- **THEN** the session has the workspaces `swb-1`, `swb-2`, and a units workspace for each team with work in flight

### Requirement: The conductor waits for a stage through crew
After starting a unit's stage or a fix, the conductor SHALL wait for it through crew (`crew unit wait <unit>`, `crew chore wait <chore>`, `crew fix <team> <name> --wait`), run as a background command, and SHALL NOT wait on an agent with herdr directly or read a pane to learn an outcome. The wait SHALL run on the team's host and return when the stage has ended, is stuck, or its timeout passes, and say which.

#### Scenario: A stage settles
- **WHEN** a conductor has `crew unit wait <unit>` running, and the unit's verify agent has saved its report and settled
- **THEN** the command returns naming the report's path, and the stage's end is in the run record

#### Scenario: A quiet agent with nothing delivered
- **WHEN** a verify agent ends its turn to wait on suites it started in the background, with no report saved yet
- **THEN** the wait does not return, and returns once the report is saved and the agent has stopped working

#### Scenario: The timeout passes
- **WHEN** the stage has neither ended nor become stuck when the timeout passes
- **THEN** the command returns saying the stage is still running, and whether its agent is working or waiting on work of its own, and no `stage.end` entry is written

### Requirement: A unit in flight goes back through construct and review
`crew unit run <unit> construct "<words>"` SHALL be accepted at any stage before the unit has merged into its bolt. Run on a unit that is in code or in verify, it SHALL mark the unit amended in the plan before the stage starts, and only the conductor of the team holding the unit's bolt or the user SHALL be accepted to run it. Run on a unit whose intent was amended and whose construct has not run since, it SHALL be accepted from any caller. While construct runs again, `crew unit run <unit> code`, `verify` and `merge` SHALL be refused; once it has committed, the unit SHALL go on from its tasks with no approval asked, and its revised change SHALL be due for the next batch.

#### Scenario: The user wants a change to a unit in code
- **WHEN** the conductor runs `crew unit run <unit> construct "<the user's words>"` on a unit in code
- **THEN** the unit is marked amended, a fresh construct agent rewrites the change in the unit's slot and place, and when it commits the unit's stage is read from its tasks

#### Scenario: Code on an amended unit
- **WHEN** `crew unit run <unit> code` runs on a unit marked amended whose construct has not committed again
- **THEN** it is refused until that construct has committed

#### Scenario: An amended intent arrives from an approved proposal
- **WHEN** an approved proposal amends the intent of a unit in flight
- **THEN** the approval starts construct again on the unit, and the fresh construct agent is given the new intent, told the change is to be revised to it, and given the case of the proposal the user approved; the conductor is told that construct runs again and to wait for it, and the unit goes on from its tasks once it has committed, with no approval asked

#### Scenario: The planner approves on the user's word
- **WHEN** the planner runs `crew plan approve <n>` for a proposal that amends a unit in flight
- **THEN** construct starts on the unit, recorded as started by the planner, and is not refused for who asked

#### Scenario: A merged unit
- **WHEN** `crew unit run <unit> construct` runs on a unit that has merged into its bolt
- **THEN** it is refused; a defect is a fix, and a new need is a new unit

### Requirement: A worktree goes when its work is over
crew SHALL remove each worktree it made, with its branch, once its work is over and no slot holds it: a bolt's once no plan has the bolt; a unit's once its bolt holds its change archived or it has landed, or no plan has it, never while the bolt holds its change open; a chore's once its bolt or main holds its trailer, or no plan has it; a fix's once it has merged into its bolt or landed on main, its conductor dropped it, or no plan has its bolt. A fix's bolt SHALL be the one its branch tracks, the host's `bolt/<bolt>` or GitHub's `origin/bolt/<bolt>`, and whether it has merged SHALL be read against that same branch; once that branch is gone, whether the fix has landed SHALL be read from the kit's main. A fix's branch that no worktree and no slot holds SHALL go once the fix has merged or landed, or its bolt is in no plan and the kit's main holds its tip; such a branch holding anything its bolt and main lack SHALL be left as it is. A unit's or chore's branch SHALL go with its place only when `git cherry` finds no commit on it whose patch its bolt lacks, or, once the bolt has landed or the bolt's branch is gone, whose patch the kit's main as crew reads it now lacks; it SHALL never be judged by which commits are ancestors, since a unit's commits are rebased as they merge. A branch with such commits, or one git cannot compare, SHALL be kept, and the command SHALL name it with the commit it is at, how many commits it holds whose patch its bolt or main lacks, and that it is the user's to keep or delete. A dropped fix's branch SHALL be kept and named when `git cherry` finds commits whose patch its bolt lacks, or cannot compare it with its bolt, and SHALL then be the user's; one holding nothing beyond its bolt SHALL go. A fix's branch whose bolt has landed, or whose bolt's branch is gone, SHALL be judged against the kit's main as a unit's is. Each branch kept SHALL be recorded once, in a run-record entry on the team's host naming the branch, the commit it is at and how many commits it holds of its own. A bolt's branch crew made, which crew marks in the kit's git config when it makes it, SHALL go once no plan has the bolt and no worktree is on it, and a landed bolt's only when `git cherry` finds no commit on it whose patch the kit's main lacks, being otherwise kept, named, recorded and the user's; a deletion git or sluice refuses SHALL keep the branch and name why. It SHALL do this each time it reads a team, for the team's kit checkout, at once after `crew bolt drop`, `crew unit drop`, `crew chore drop`, `crew unit free`, `crew chore free` or `crew bolt land`, and at once when a wait sees a unit's, chore's or fix's merge into its bolt delivered.

#### Scenario: A dropped bolt
- **WHEN** the planner drops a bolt that swb-2 holds, none of whose units' or fixes' branches holds a commit of its own
- **THEN** `bolt/<bolt>` is gone from swb-2's host, and so are the places, branches and slots of the bolt's units and fixes

#### Scenario: A dropped bolt whose unit holds a written change
- **WHEN** the planner drops a bolt that swb-2 holds, and one of its units' branches holds the unit's change committed, which the bolt does not
- **THEN** that unit's place and slot are gone and its branch is kept, the drop's output naming it, the commit it is at and that it holds 1 commit whose patch the bolt lacks, and a run-record entry names the branch; the bolt's other places and branches go

#### Scenario: A dropped unit holding nothing of its own
- **WHEN** the planner drops a unit whose branch holds no commit beyond its bolt
- **THEN** its place and its branch are removed, and crew says the commit the branch was at

#### Scenario: Work dropped with commits of its own
- **WHEN** the planner drops a unit whose branch holds its change committed, which neither its bolt nor main has
- **THEN** its place is removed and its slot freed, its branch is kept, and the drop's output names the branch, the commit it is at and how many commits it holds whose patch the bolt lacks

#### Scenario: A unit whose commits reached the bolt rebased
- **WHEN** a unit's branch holds commits whose patches the bolt holds under other hashes, rebased as they merged, and its place goes
- **THEN** its branch is removed, since `git cherry` finds nothing of its own on it

#### Scenario: A unit whose bolt's branch is gone
- **WHEN** a unit's place goes after its bolt's branch was removed, and its branch holds a commit whose patch the kit's main lacks
- **THEN** its branch is kept and named, compared with the kit's main

#### Scenario: A unit of a landed bolt
- **WHEN** a unit's place goes after its bolt has landed, while the bolt's branch still stands, and its branch holds a commit whose patch the bolt holds and main lacks
- **THEN** its branch is kept and named, compared with the kit's main and not with the bolt

#### Scenario: A slot freed by hand
- **WHEN** `crew unit free u` frees a slot holding a unit no longer in its team's bolt, whose branch holds a commit of its own
- **THEN** the slot is freed and the place removed, and `unit/u` is kept and named

#### Scenario: A leftover from before
- **WHEN** a kit checkout has the worktree of a bolt that no plan has, and a place of a unit that has merged, which no slot holds
- **THEN** the next `crew status` of a team building in that checkout removes both, with their branches

#### Scenario: A bolt branch crew did not make
- **WHEN** a kit checkout has a `bolt/` branch with no mark of crew's, of a bolt no plan has
- **THEN** crew leaves it as it is

#### Scenario: The host does not answer
- **WHEN** a bolt is dropped while its team's host cannot be reached
- **THEN** the plan changes, crew says the host's worktrees were not removed, and the team's next read removes them, keeping each branch that holds work of its own

#### Scenario: A unit carried onto its bolt
- **WHEN** a unit's change is open on its bolt, carried there inside another unit's merge, and the team is read
- **THEN** its worktree, its branch and its slot are kept

#### Scenario: A dropped fix's place with uncommitted changes
- **WHEN** a fix is dropped while its place holds a file nobody committed
- **THEN** its place and branch are kept, and every read names them until the place is clean, when crew removes them

#### Scenario: A landed fix's place and branch
- **WHEN** a fix's place stands, no slot holds it, and its bolt has landed with the kit's main holding the fix's tip
- **THEN** the next read of a team building in that checkout removes the place and the branch, saying the fix has landed on main

#### Scenario: A landed fix's branch with no place
- **WHEN** a kit checkout holds a `fix/` branch whose place is gone, which no slot holds, whose bolt has landed and been removed, and whose tip the kit's main holds
- **THEN** the next read of a team building in that checkout removes the branch, saying the commit it was at

#### Scenario: A fix branch with work nobody holds
- **WHEN** a `fix/` branch with no place and no slot holds a commit neither its bolt nor the kit's main has, and its bolt is in no plan
- **THEN** crew leaves the branch as it is

#### Scenario: A fix that tracks its bolt on GitHub
- **WHEN** a fix's branch tracks `origin/bolt/<bolt>` and that branch holds the fix's tip
- **THEN** crew reads the fix's bolt as `<bolt>` and the fix as merged, and frees its slot and removes its place and branch as for one tracking the host's `bolt/<bolt>`

#### Scenario: A merge lands
- **WHEN** the conductor's `crew unit wait a` sees unit `a`'s merge into its bolt delivered, its agent idle
- **THEN** the wait's answer says its slot is free and its place and branch removed, before the conductor reads the team again

### Requirement: Uncommitted changes keep a worktree
A worktree whose work is over SHALL be kept, with its branch, while it has uncommitted changes: modified tracked files, or untracked files git does not ignore. Each read of the team SHALL name it, what its work was and that its changes are the user's to keep or discard, until it is clean, when crew removes it, or gone. Files git ignores SHALL NOT keep a worktree, and go with it.

#### Scenario: A merged unit with a stray file
- **WHEN** a unit has merged into its bolt and its place has an untracked file git does not ignore
- **THEN** the unit's slot is freed, its place and branch are kept, and every `crew status` of the team names the place as kept for its uncommitted changes

#### Scenario: The user clears the changes
- **WHEN** the stray file is removed from a kept place
- **THEN** the next `crew status` of the team removes the place and its branch

#### Scenario: Installed dependencies
- **WHEN** a merged unit's place holds only files git ignores, such as installed dependencies
- **THEN** the place is removed with them

### Requirement: crew removes only its own worktrees
crew SHALL remove only worktrees directly under `<kit>/places/` on a `unit/`, `chore/` or `fix/` branch, and the worktree a bolt given before bolts were branches still has, directly under `<kit>/bolts/` on its `bolt/` branch, and only bolt branches it marked as its own. It SHALL never remove the main checkout, a worktree elsewhere, one on another branch or on none, or a locked one, which it names as kept. It SHALL remove nothing when it cannot read every plan the kit's work goes in.

#### Scenario: A worktree made by hand
- **WHEN** a kit checkout has a worktree beside `main` that the user made, on a branch of their own
- **THEN** crew leaves it as it is

#### Scenario: A merge partway through its rebase
- **WHEN** a place is on no branch because a rebase stopped partway
- **THEN** crew leaves it as it is

#### Scenario: A plan that can't be read
- **WHEN** crew reads a team while the plan of a partition whose teams build the kit cannot be fetched
- **THEN** no worktree is removed, and crew says why

### Requirement: A conductor carries a stage's design questions together
When a stage stops short on design questions, its conductor SHALL send the questions that are linked (from one stage, or from units of its bolt about the same thing) to the design agent in one message, in the words of whoever asked. When the design agent asks for a reading before it rules, the conductor SHALL get it from its team's ops and send it back with the questions. The conductor SHALL bring the answer back to the stage verbatim, with the documentation or reading it names. When the planner says it holds corrections to a unit whose construct is running, the conductor SHALL tell the planner when that construct settles.

#### Scenario: A construct stops with several questions
- **WHEN** a unit's construct stops short, naming three design questions about the same service
- **THEN** the conductor sends all three to the design agent in one message, and the construct gets the answer back verbatim, with the reading it names

#### Scenario: The design agent needs a reading
- **WHEN** the design agent answers that it needs the live policy before it can rule
- **THEN** the conductor asks its team's ops for that reading, and sends it to the design agent with the questions it was for

#### Scenario: The planner is holding corrections
- **WHEN** the planner has said it holds corrections to a unit, and that unit's construct settles
- **THEN** the conductor tells the planner, which then proposes the one amendment

### Requirement: A stage ends at its deliverable
Each stage SHALL have one deliverable, read in the kit or on the team's host and never kept by hand. A stage SHALL end when its deliverable exists and its agent has stopped working, or when its agent stops short through crew, and SHALL NOT end because its agent has gone quiet.

#### Scenario: Delivered while still working
- **WHEN** a code agent's last task is committed and the agent is still writing its summary
- **THEN** the stage ends once the agent stops working, so the next stage never starts over a working agent

### Requirement: The deliverable of each stage
The deliverables SHALL be: construct, the unit's change committed since the stage began, its planning complete; code, a commit since the stage began with every task ticked at the branch's head; verify, a report for the unit saved in the team's reports folder since the stage began; merge, the bolt holding the unit's change archived, or the fix's commits; a fix's code, a commit on its branch since it began. A line a proof of ops's left in a team's stages file from before SHALL be dropped, owed nothing.

#### Scenario: Construct
- **WHEN** a construct agent commits its change with its planning complete and stops working
- **THEN** the wait returns saying the change is committed, naming the branch's head

#### Scenario: Code with tasks still open
- **WHEN** a code agent stops working with one task of four still open at its branch's head, and has said nothing it needs
- **THEN** the stage has not ended

#### Scenario: Code run again on a ticked change
- **WHEN** code is run with a verify report's path, its one round of fixes, on a unit whose tasks were all ticked before it began
- **THEN** the stage ends only once a commit has been made since it began

#### Scenario: A verify run again
- **WHEN** verify runs again with no commit since the last verify, whose report is still in the folder
- **THEN** that older report is not this run's deliverable, and the stage ends at the report this run saves

#### Scenario: Merge
- **WHEN** a merge agent archives the unit's change, its submission lands the unit on its bolt, and the agent stops working
- **THEN** the wait returns naming the bolt's head that holds the unit

#### Scenario: A change merged open
- **WHEN** a merge agent lands the unit on its bolt with its change not archived
- **THEN** the stage has not ended, and the wait says the bolt holds the change open

#### Scenario: A proof from before
- **WHEN** a team's stages file holds a proof line ops's proof left before this change
- **THEN** the team's next read drops it, records no end for it, and owes nothing

### Requirement: A stage's agent says what it needs through crew
A stage's agent that cannot finish without something only someone else can give SHALL say so with `crew needs "<what it needs>"`, which ends its stage short. crew SHALL carry the agent's words to the conductor: in the answer of a wait running on the stage, otherwise in a tell marked as from that agent. crew SHALL refuse `crew needs` from anyone but the agent of a stage crew owes an end, and with no words.

#### Scenario: A coder pauses
- **WHEN** a code agent runs `crew needs "a decision on which table holds tenants"` while the conductor waits on its unit
- **THEN** the wait returns saying the stage stopped short, with those words, and the stage's end is recorded

#### Scenario: Nobody is waiting
- **WHEN** a stage's agent runs `crew needs` and no wait is running on its stage
- **THEN** the conductor is told the agent's words, marked as from that agent

#### Scenario: Not a stage's agent
- **WHEN** a conductor, or a slot's agent whose stage has already ended, runs `crew needs "x"`
- **THEN** crew refuses, saying the caller runs no stage crew is waiting on

### Requirement: A stage quiet too long is reported stuck
A wait SHALL report a stage stuck, naming the stage and its agent, when the agent has not been working for longer than crew's limit (15 minutes unless the wait is given another), with no deliverable, no stop-short, and no background work of its own still running. A stuck report SHALL end neither the stage nor its agent. When crew cannot read whether the agent waits on work of its own, the clock SHALL run and the report SHALL say what could not be read.

#### Scenario: A question left in a pane
- **WHEN** a construct agent asks a question in its pane and stays quiet for longer than the limit, with nothing committed
- **THEN** the wait returns saying the stage is stuck, naming the agent and how long it has been quiet

#### Scenario: Suites still running
- **WHEN** a verify agent has been quiet for longer than the limit while suites it started in the background still run
- **THEN** the stage is not reported stuck

#### Scenario: The agent is gone
- **WHEN** the stage's agent is no longer running and nothing was delivered or said
- **THEN** the wait returns at once saying so, and records nothing

### Requirement: A worktree goes through the kit's remove hooks
crew SHALL remove each worktree it removes through the kit's own worktree tool, run from the kit's main checkout, so the kit's remove hooks run: those before removal with the worktree's files still there, those after it once it is gone. crew SHALL NOT skip the kit's hooks, nor approve them for the user. A kit with no remove hooks SHALL see its worktrees go as before, with no approval asked.

#### Scenario: A kit that tears down what it stood up
- **WHEN** a merged fix's place is removed in a kit whose remove hooks tear down what it stood up for that worktree, approved on that host
- **THEN** the hook that runs before removal finds the place's files, the place is then removed, and the hook that runs after removal runs

#### Scenario: A kit with merge hooks only
- **WHEN** a merged unit's place is removed in a kit whose worktree config has only merge hooks
- **THEN** the place and its branch go as before, and no approval is asked

### Requirement: A remove hook that fails keeps the worktree
A worktree whose removal the kit's worktree tool refuses, because a remove hook that runs before removal failed or because the user has not approved the kit's hooks on that host, SHALL be kept with its branch. Each read of the team SHALL name it with what the tool said failed, never the tool's advice to skip hooks or prompts, and SHALL try it again, removing it once the tool accepts.

#### Scenario: The kit's hook fails
- **WHEN** a merged unit's place is removed and the kit's hook before removal exits non-zero
- **THEN** the place and its branch are kept, and `crew status` of the team names the place and the hook that failed

#### Scenario: The hook is fixed
- **WHEN** the cause of a failed remove hook is cleared
- **THEN** the next `crew status` of the team runs the hook again, removes the place, and decides its branch

#### Scenario: Hooks not yet approved on the host
- **WHEN** a kit has remove hooks the user has not approved on the team's host
- **THEN** no hook runs, the worktree and its branch are kept, and crew names the worktree and says the kit's hooks wait for the user's approval on that host

### Requirement: The branch is crew's to decide, never the worktree tool's
When crew removes a worktree through the kit's worktree tool, the tool SHALL leave the worktree's branch alone, and crew SHALL then keep or remove the branch by its own judgment. When crew cannot tell from the tool that the branch was left alone, it SHALL keep the branch and say so.

#### Scenario: A branch the tool would have kept
- **WHEN** a landed bolt's worktree is removed and the tool would judge its branch unmerged
- **THEN** the branch is kept or removed as crew's own rule says, and crew's message names what became of it

#### Scenario: The tool's answer can't be read
- **WHEN** the tool removes a worktree but crew cannot read what it says of the branch
- **THEN** crew says the worktree is removed and its branch kept, because crew could not tell what became of it

### Requirement: A landing records the commit main holds
`crew bolt land` SHALL record, in its `bolt.land` entry of the run record, the kit the bolt landed on and the commit that kit's main holds on the team's host once the bolt has landed, and SHALL be refused when it can't read that commit.

#### Scenario: The landing's commit
- **WHEN** main-level ops has merged swb-1's bolt into switchboard-kit's main, pushed it, and runs `crew bolt land`
- **THEN** the landing's entry names switchboard-kit and the commit its main holds, and crew says the bolt has landed at that commit

### Requirement: A bolt no team holds lands once its units are on main
`crew bolt land` SHALL land a bolt no team holds, on the user's word, once its kit's main holds each of its units' changes, open or archived, and each of its chores, read on the host and main checkout of a team of the partition building that kit, recording the landing as any landing; it SHALL be refused for such a bolt with no units or chores, whose kit no team of the partition builds, or while any unit's change is not on main.

#### Scenario: A bolt built outside a team
- **WHEN** the plan holds the bolt `deploys-go-through-crew`, no team holds it, and each of its units' changes is archived on crew's main
- **THEN** `crew bolt land deploys-go-through-crew` removes it and its units from the plan, and the landing's entry names crew and the commit its main holds

#### Scenario: A unit not yet on main
- **WHEN** one unit of a bolt no team holds has no change on its kit's main
- **THEN** `crew bolt land` is refused, naming the unit and its stage

### Requirement: A verify's report says what to do with each finding
A unit's verify SHALL run once, when its tasks are all ticked, and a chore's once its code has committed. It SHALL check the code against the unit's change as written, its proposal, specs, design and tasks, or a chore's against its words, and its report SHALL end by marking each finding it raised fix or leave. Nothing from verify SHALL go to the planner or wait on the user.

#### Scenario: A report's last section
- **WHEN** unit `u`'s verify raises a critical finding, a warning and a suggestion
- **THEN** its saved report ends with a line for each, marked fix or leave, and the critical one is not marked leave

### Requirement: A verify's findings are fixed in one round, with no card
The conductor SHALL take a report's marks as they stand, post no card for it and ask the user nothing about it. When the report marks a finding fix, the conductor SHALL run code once more, a fresh agent told the report's path, which fixes every finding marked fix and only those; a finding marked leave SHALL be left. No verify SHALL run after the fix round, and the unit then merges. A fix round that stops short or fails SHALL be a stuck stage, run again or passed over only on the user's word.

#### Scenario: A report with findings to fix
- **WHEN** unit `u`'s verify saves a report that marks two findings fix and one leave
- **THEN** the conductor runs `crew unit run u code "Fix every finding the verify report at <path> marks fix."` with no card posted, the coder fixes those two and not the third, and once that stage delivers the conductor runs the merge with no verify again

#### Scenario: A report with nothing to fix
- **WHEN** unit `u`'s report marks no finding fix
- **THEN** the conductor runs its merge next

#### Scenario: A fix round that stops short
- **WHEN** the coder of `u`'s fix round stops short, saying a finding marked fix needs a task reworded
- **THEN** the conductor tells the user it is stuck, and runs it again or merges `u` only on the user's word

### Requirement: A unit fixed after its verify waits to merge
A unit that has delivered its verify, and its fix round when one ran, SHALL wait to merge: a forecast or an amended `After` unit reaches it as a settle stage, and a chore's merge needs its verify once. Construct run again SHALL end the wait, since its change is new.

#### Scenario: A forecast after the fix round
- **WHEN** a forecast against the bolt reaches unit `u`, which delivered its verify and then its fix round, and no stage of it runs
- **THEN** it reaches `u` as a settle stage

### Requirement: A unit's merge archives its change first
A unit's merge stage SHALL archive its change, `openspec archive <unit> --yes` in its place, committed on its branch, before it merges, and crew SHALL read the merge as delivered only once the bolt holds the change archived. A place whose change its merge archived SHALL be read from the archive, its tasks, Touches and planning, so the unit reads as waiting to merge. A chore and a fix have no change to archive.

#### Scenario: Archived and merged
- **WHEN** the merge agent of unit `u` archives `u`'s change, commits it, and merges `unit/u` into `bolt/y`
- **THEN** `bolt/y` holds `openspec/changes/archive/<date>-u`, `u` reads merged, and the merge stage has delivered

#### Scenario: Archived, not yet merged
- **WHEN** `u`'s place holds its change archived and `bolt/y` does not hold `u` yet
- **THEN** `crew bolts` shows `u` in verify, its tasks read from the archive

#### Scenario: An archive openspec refuses
- **WHEN** `openspec archive u --yes` refuses a delta that modifies a requirement a sibling unit adds and has not merged
- **THEN** the merge agent stops, saying so with openspec's words, and nothing is merged

### Requirement: A bolt's proof is its verification
A bolt SHALL be proven by its verification alone: sluice's verdict on its last landing, green at its head. Nobody SHALL deploy the bolt or work a list from its changes to prove it; ops SHALL prove and deploy nothing. The conductor SHALL offer the bolt for landing once every unit and chore has merged and its verification is green.

#### Scenario: Ready to land
- **WHEN** every unit of a bolt has merged and `crew bolts` shows its verification green
- **THEN** the conductor tells the user the bolt is ready to land and posts its landing card, and no one is asked to prove it

#### Scenario: Not yet green
- **WHEN** every unit of a bolt has merged and its head is no landing of sluice's
- **THEN** the bolt is not offered for landing, and `crew bolts` says what its verification is

### Requirement: Every agent crew starts carries its name for sluice
Every agent crew starts SHALL have `SLUICE_AGENT` in its environment, the same as its `CREW_AGENT`, from its start and again when herdr resumes it where crew gives it its identity back; a main level's agent herdr resumes off its main level's host SHALL have neither (`main-level`).

#### Scenario: A stage's agent starts
- **WHEN** crew starts `swb-1-unit-2` for a code stage
- **THEN** its environment holds `CREW_AGENT=swb-1-unit-2` and `SLUICE_AGENT=swb-1-unit-2`

#### Scenario: herdr resumes an agent
- **WHEN** herdr resumes the conductor's session without crew's environment
- **THEN** its commands see `SLUICE_AGENT` back, the same as its `CREW_AGENT`

#### Scenario: herdr resumes a main level's agent on another host
- **WHEN** herdr resumes `swancloud-planner`'s session on a host the teams file does not name for swancloud's main level
- **THEN** its commands see neither `CREW_AGENT` nor `SLUICE_AGENT`

### Requirement: Ahead in a bolt is its merge order
Where crew tells a unit of the units ahead of it in its bolt, in a code stage's note of overlapping Touches and in the forecasts on a conductor's wait, a unit `a` SHALL be ahead of `b` when `b` comes after `a` through `After` at any depth, or, when neither comes after the other, when the plan lists `a` first. A unit's dependent SHALL never be named ahead of it, nor a unit forecast against one that comes after it.

#### Scenario: A dependent listed first
- **WHEN** unit `d` comes after unit `s`, the plan lists `d` before `s`, and their Touches overlap
- **THEN** `s`'s code is told nothing of `d`, and `d`'s code is told that `s` is ahead of it and not merged

#### Scenario: A forecast between them
- **WHEN** the conductor's wait finds `d`'s and `s`'s heads in conflict
- **THEN** the forecast reaches `d`, against `s`, and no `After` is written, since `d` already comes after `s`

#### Scenario: At any depth
- **WHEN** `c` comes after `b`, which comes after `a`, and the plan lists `c` first
- **THEN** `a` is ahead of `c`, and a forecast between them reaches `c` with no `After` written

#### Scenario: Units neither comes after
- **WHEN** units `x` and `y` share no `After`, and the plan lists `x` first
- **THEN** `x` is ahead of `y`

### Requirement: A unit carried onto its bolt keeps its own merge
A unit whose change its bolt holds open, carried there inside another unit's merge, SHALL NOT read merged: `crew status` SHALL name it carried by another's merge, its own merge owed, on its slot's line or a line of its own. Its merge stage SHALL make its place again from the bolt when it is gone, and the merge SHALL archive its change there and deliver once the bolt holds it archived.

#### Scenario: Named in the team's status
- **WHEN** unit `d`'s change is open on its bolt, carried there in `s`'s merge, every task ticked, and its slot holds it
- **THEN** `crew status` shows `d  verify 2/2  carried by another's merge, its own merge owed`

#### Scenario: Its place gone
- **WHEN** `d`'s place and branch were removed, and the conductor runs `crew unit run d merge`
- **THEN** `d`'s place is made again on `unit/d` from the bolt, its merge archives the change there and submits, and the stage delivers once the bolt holds the change archived

### Requirement: A merge carries no other unit's open change
crew SHALL refuse a unit's or chore's merge before it starts while its branch holds another unit's change of the plan open that its bolt does not hold archived, naming each such change, and saying to take out of the branch the work of a unit that comes after the merging one and to merge any other first. The merging unit's own change, a change the bolt holds archived and a change no unit of the plan has SHALL NOT count.

#### Scenario: A dependency holding its dependent's work
- **WHEN** unit `s`'s branch holds unit `d`'s change open, `d` comes after `s`, and the bolt does not hold `d`'s change archived
- **THEN** `crew unit run s merge` is refused, naming `d` and saying to take its work out of `unit/s`

#### Scenario: A unit it does not come after
- **WHEN** a chore's branch holds unit `x`'s change open, and the bolt does not hold it archived
- **THEN** `crew chore run <chore> merge` is refused, saying to merge `x` first

#### Scenario: A dependent's place holding what it took
- **WHEN** unit `d`'s place took `s`'s work, and `s` has since merged with its change archived on the bolt
- **THEN** `d`'s merge is not refused for it

#### Scenario: A change main has long held open
- **WHEN** every branch holds a change open that main has held since before archives, which no unit of the plan has
- **THEN** no merge is refused for it

### Requirement: A bolt's units are constructed in waves
Once a team holds a bolt, the conductor SHALL run construct, as slots allow and in the file's order, for every unit whose `After` units have all finished construct. A unit's construct SHALL NOT wait on the user, on another unit's merge, or on another unit's Touches. Changes are so written in waves: the units with no `After` first, then each unit as soon as the changes it builds on are committed.

#### Scenario: A bolt is given
- **WHEN** a team with four slots is given a bolt of three units, C with `After: A`
- **THEN** A's and B's constructs run at once, and C's starts as soon as A's change is committed

#### Scenario: More units than slots
- **WHEN** a team with two slots holds a bolt of five units with no `After`
- **THEN** two constructs run, and the next unit in the file's order is constructed as each slot frees

### Requirement: A dependent builds on its After units' work as it stands
When a unit's place is made, crew SHALL bring into `unit/<unit>` the head of each of its `After` units that has not merged, as that head stands. When an `After` unit merges into the bolt, crew SHALL rebase the dependent's place onto the bolt, leaving out that unit's work as the place took it, only while the dependent's agent is not working and its place has no uncommitted changes. A rebase that conflicts SHALL be undone and handed to the dependent as a conflict is.

#### Scenario: Construct on a constructed dependency
- **WHEN** B has `After: A`, A's change is committed and A is in code, and B's construct starts
- **THEN** B's place holds A's commits as they stood when B's place was made

#### Scenario: The dependency merges
- **WHEN** A merges into the bolt while B's agent is idle
- **THEN** B's place is rebased onto the bolt, and holds no commit of A's that the bolt already holds

#### Scenario: The dependency settled before it merged
- **WHEN** A's settle rewrote A's change, which B's place holds as it took it, and A merges into the bolt while B's agent is idle
- **THEN** B's place is rebased onto the bolt with only B's own commits replayed, and nothing conflicts on A's files

#### Scenario: The dependent's own line meets the dependency's settled one
- **WHEN** B's own commit changed the line A's settle rewrote, and A merges into the bolt while B's agent is idle
- **THEN** B's rebase conflicts on that line and is undone, and B is told the conflicting paths with a command that rebases it the way crew's rebase did, leaving out A's work as B's place took it

#### Scenario: The dependent is working
- **WHEN** A merges into the bolt while B's agent is working
- **THEN** B's place is not touched until the agent is idle

### Requirement: A dependent is told when its After unit is amended
When an `After` unit is amended after its dependent's code has started, the dependent SHALL NOT be held. crew SHALL tell the dependent of the amendment, with the path of the revised change, the way a forecast reaches it.

#### Scenario: The dependency is amended
- **WHEN** B has `After: A`, B is in code, and A is constructed again with the user's notes
- **THEN** B's code goes on, and B is told that A's change was revised, with its path

### Requirement: Touches is a list crew reads
A unit's `proposal.md` SHALL end with a `## Touches` heading holding one fenced block of repository paths or globs, one to a line. crew SHALL read it before the unit's code starts, and SHALL refuse code when the heading or the block is missing or empty.

#### Scenario: No Touches
- **WHEN** a unit's proposal has no `## Touches` block
- **THEN** its code is refused, saying the change must declare what it touches

### Requirement: Overlapping Touches are told before code
When a unit's code is about to start and its Touches overlap those of an unmerged unit ahead of it in the bolt, crew SHALL tell the later unit which entries overlap and where the other unit's change is. Overlap SHALL NOT hold the unit's code.

#### Scenario: Two units declare the same file
- **WHEN** units A and B, A first in the file, both list `plugin/lib/plan.py`, and B's code is about to start
- **THEN** B's code starts, and its prompt names the overlap with A on `plugin/lib/plan.py` and the path of A's change

### Requirement: Verify names what a unit changed and did not declare
At verify, every path `git diff --name-only bolt/<bolt>...unit/<unit>` names that no entry of the unit's Touches matches SHALL be a finding of the verify report. The code stage that acts on the finding SHALL correct the Touches block of `proposal.md`, and change nothing else of the change.

#### Scenario: A path not declared
- **WHEN** a unit changed `README.md` against its bolt and its Touches do not match it
- **THEN** the verify report names `README.md` as changed and not declared, and the code stage run on the finding adds it to the Touches block

### Requirement: A conflict is forecast on the conductor's waits
Each time the conductor waits on a stage or a proof through crew, crew SHALL dry-run, with `git merge-tree`, the merge of each unmerged unit's head with `bolt/<bolt>` and with the head of each unmerged unit ahead of it in the bolt's merge order, writing no ref. A dependent SHALL be compared with a unit it comes after only on what it adds beyond the head of that unit its place took.

#### Scenario: The dry run moves nothing
- **WHEN** the conductor's wait forecasts the merges of three unmerged units
- **THEN** no branch of the kit has moved

#### Scenario: A dependent carries its After unit's rewritten work
- **WHEN** B, after A, holds A's work as its place took it, A is rebased onto the bolt and its settle revises A's own file, and the conductor's wait runs
- **THEN** B is forecast nothing against A for that file, and a line B's own commit changed that A's settle also changed is forecast

### Requirement: A forecast reaches the later unit once
A predicted conflict SHALL reach the later unit once for the same two heads, with the conflicting paths and the other unit's OpenSpec change: told to its agent once the agent is not working, when its stage is running; carried in the prompt of its next stage, when no agent of it is running; or as a settle stage, when it has delivered and waits to merge. Overlap SHALL order merging only, and SHALL NOT hold any unit's construct, code or verify.

#### Scenario: Two units will conflict while one codes
- **WHEN** A and B, A first, both edit the same lines of `plugin/lib/plan.py`, B's code agent is running, and the conductor's wait runs
- **THEN** B's agent is told, when idle, that its merge after A is forecast to conflict on `plugin/lib/plan.py`, with A's change, and A is told nothing

#### Scenario: No agent running
- **WHEN** a conflict is forecast for a unit in `planned` with no agent in its slot
- **THEN** its code stage's prompt carries the forecast

#### Scenario: Nothing new
- **WHEN** the conductor's wait runs again and neither head has moved
- **THEN** nothing is told again

#### Scenario: A newer forecast against the bolt
- **WHEN** a unit with no agent running was forecast to conflict with the bolt, the bolt moved, and a newer forecast against the bolt was made for it
- **THEN** its next stage's prompt carries only the newer one

### Requirement: A forecast conflict between two units orders them
When a conflict is forecast between two units that have both not merged, the forecast SHALL reach the later unit, and the later unit's agent SHALL take the earlier unit's work into its place and resolve the conflict there; the forecast alone SHALL NOT give the later unit `After` on the earlier. crew SHALL put the later unit after the earlier one, in the conductor's name, only when the later unit's settle delivers a commit whose branch holds the earlier unit's work, while both are unmerged, and at most once for a pair; a settle that finds nothing to change SHALL order nothing. An `After` crew wrote for a pair whose later unit's settle then stopped short with no commit, and whose branch does not hold the other's work, SHALL be lifted by crew itself, on the conductor's wait that sees that end or on any wait after. An `After` crew wrote for a pair that anyone else lifted SHALL NOT be written again. No proposal or card SHALL ask the user to lift an `After` crew wrote from a forecast.

#### Scenario: Two unmerged units
- **WHEN** a conflict is forecast between A and B, A first, neither merged
- **THEN** the forecast reaches B with A's change, B's agent is told to take A's work into B's place and resolve the conflict there, and the forecast alone gives B no `After`

#### Scenario: The settle takes the other's work
- **WHEN** B, verified and waiting to merge, is forecast to conflict with A, and B's settle delivers a commit that merges A's work into B's place
- **THEN** B gains `After: A` in the conductor's name, and B's merge waits for A's

#### Scenario: The settle finds nothing to change
- **WHEN** B's settle for a forecast against A stops short saying nothing needs to change
- **THEN** B gains no `After`, and B merges without waiting for A

#### Scenario: An order crew wrote is lifted by crew
- **WHEN** B holds `After: A` that crew wrote from a forecast, B's settle for it stopped short with no commit, and B's branch does not hold A's work
- **THEN** the conductor's next wait lifts B's `After: A` in the conductor's name, and no proposal or card is made for it

#### Scenario: An order the user lifted stays lifted
- **WHEN** crew wrote B's `After: A`, the user lifted it by proposal, and a later forecast on new heads finds B and A in conflict again
- **THEN** the forecast reaches B, and crew does not write `After: A` back

#### Scenario: One order for a pair
- **WHEN** crew put B after A on a settle's commit, someone lifted it, and a later settle of B takes A's work again
- **THEN** crew does not write `After: A` again

### Requirement: A unit waiting to merge settles
A unit that has delivered its verify and waits to merge, and that a forecast conflict or an amended `After` unit reaches, SHALL be given a settle stage: a fresh agent in its slot, with the coder's brief, prompted with the forecast or the amendment. The stage's deliverable SHALL be a commit on `unit/<unit>` made since the stage began; when nothing needs to change, its agent SHALL stop short saying so, and the unit goes on to merge.

#### Scenario: A verified unit is forecast to conflict
- **WHEN** B is verified and waits to merge, and a conflict with the bolt is forecast for it
- **THEN** a settle stage starts in B's slot with the forecast, and ends at the commit that resolves it

### Requirement: A conflict is resolved by the later unit's agent
A conflict between two units, forecast, met at a rebase or met at merge, SHALL be resolved in the later unit's place by the later unit's agent, which SHALL be given the other unit's OpenSpec change and the conflicting paths, and SHALL keep both changes' intents. The earlier unit SHALL NOT be changed for it.

#### Scenario: A merge conflicts
- **WHEN** B's merge into the bolt conflicts with A, which merged first
- **THEN** B's agent resolves it in B's place with A's change in hand, and B merges again

### Requirement: A construct reads every source it is handed, and what each points at
A construct SHALL read each of its unit's sources, and SHALL open what each points at: a page or file it names, an artifact's link, a branch. It SHALL read a page as committed on its repository's main when the stage starts, not the bolt's copy in its own worktree, and a signal with `crew signal show`. A construct run again SHALL read the sources afresh.

#### Scenario: A signal points at a page in another repo
- **WHEN** a unit's sources are a signal and the signal names a page in another repo where the user's design lives
- **THEN** the construct reads the signal with `crew signal show`, opens that page, and cites both

#### Scenario: A record lands after the bolt is cut
- **WHEN** the design agent commits a record on the kit's main after the unit's bolt was cut, and the unit names that record as a source
- **THEN** the unit's construct reads the record as main holds it, not the bolt's older copy in the unit's worktree

### Requirement: A construct stops on a source it cannot open
When a construct cannot open one of its unit's sources, or something a source points at, it SHALL stop through `crew needs`, naming what it could not open and which source named it, and SHALL NOT write the change from the intent in its place.

#### Scenario: A signal names only a conversation's id
- **WHEN** a unit's source is a signal whose words point at a conversation by its id, and nothing names what that conversation made
- **THEN** the construct commits no change and stops through crew, naming the conversation and the signal

### Requirement: A construct builds from its sources where the intent says more or otherwise
Where a unit's intent says more than its sources, or says something they contradict, its construct SHALL write the change from the sources, and its final message SHALL open with the difference: what the intent says, what the sources say instead, and which source. A construct whose intent and sources agree SHALL say nothing of a difference.

#### Scenario: The intent names a measure the sources do not
- **WHEN** a unit's intent names a measure and its sample points, and the design its sources lead to shows a different measure
- **THEN** the change builds the sources' measure, and the construct's final message opens by naming the intent's measure, the sources', and the source

#### Scenario: Intent and sources agree
- **WHEN** a unit's intent names only the outcome its sources describe
- **THEN** the construct writes the change and its final message names no difference

### Requirement: A stage begins only when its agent takes its prompt
When crew starts a stage in a slot (construct, code, verify or merge of a unit, a fix, or a fix's merge), it SHALL send the stage's prompt only once the slot's new agent is ready for input: herdr reports it idle or done, it waits on no question in its pane, and herdr names its session. The stage SHALL begin only when the agent's transcript shows that Claude received the prompt after crew sent it, within a short time. Text typed into a pane and not submitted SHALL never be a stage's state.

#### Scenario: The prompt is taken
- **WHEN** `crew unit run a code` starts a fresh agent in a's slot, and the agent's transcript shows `/opsx:apply a` received moments after crew sent it
- **THEN** the command says the slot has a in code, records the stage's start, and owes its end, having sent the prompt once

#### Scenario: The agent never becomes ready
- **WHEN** a fix's fresh agent stays on a question in its pane, and never becomes ready for input within the time crew waits
- **THEN** nothing is typed into its pane, and the command fails saying the fix's stage never started because its agent was waiting on a question

### Requirement: A stage's prompt not taken is sent once more
When the agent's transcript does not show the stage's prompt received within the time, and the agent sits idle or done, crew SHALL clear what was left typed in the agent's input and send the prompt once more, and read the transcript again. crew SHALL never send a prompt again, or press anything in the pane, while the agent is working.

#### Scenario: The prompt is left typed
- **WHEN** `crew unit run a code` sends the prompt and the agent's transcript shows nothing received within the time, while the agent sits idle
- **THEN** crew clears the agent's input, sends the prompt once more, and, when the transcript then shows it received, records the stage's start as for a prompt taken at once

### Requirement: A stage whose prompt is not taken never started
When the second sending is not taken either, or the agent never becomes ready for input, crew SHALL leave nothing typed in the pane, record the stage's start as refused with the reason, owe no end for it, and fail, saying the stage never started and how to run it again. When crew cannot read whether the prompt was taken, it SHALL send nothing more, record the start as refused naming what it could not read, and fail.

#### Scenario: The second sending is not taken either
- **WHEN** neither sending of a's code prompt shows in the agent's transcript within the time
- **THEN** the agent's input is cleared, the command fails saying a's code stage never started and to run `crew unit run a code` again, the run record holds the start as refused, and `crew unit wait a` finds no stage owed

#### Scenario: crew cannot read whether it was taken
- **WHEN** a merge stage's prompt is sent, and the agent's transcript cannot be read, or the agent is working while its transcript shows no prompt received
- **THEN** the prompt is not sent again and nothing is pressed in the pane, and the command fails naming what crew could not read

### Requirement: A team taken down starts no stage
While a team is down, from `crew down` or `crew close` until `crew up` brings it back, `crew unit run <unit> <stage>` SHALL be refused for every stage, construct, code, verify and merge alike, on any unit of the team's bolt, saying `<team> is down; crew up <team> brings it back`. The refusal SHALL come before crew frees or takes a slot, writes the plan or ends an agent, and SHALL be recorded as a refused `stage.start`.

#### Scenario: Code on a team taken down
- **WHEN** `crew unit run <unit> code` runs while the unit's team is down
- **THEN** it is refused with "<team> is down; crew up <team> brings it back", the plan is not written, no slot's agent is sent anything, and the run record holds a refused `stage.start` for `stage/<unit>/code`

#### Scenario: An amendment's construct on a team taken down
- **WHEN** an amendment to a unit of a team that is down is applied
- **THEN** its construct is refused the same way, and the unit keeps its mark and reads `amended`

#### Scenario: The team is brought back
- **WHEN** `crew up <team>` brings the team back
- **THEN** `crew unit run` starts stages on its units again

### Requirement: A conductor brought back is told which units wait amended
When crew starts or brings back a team's conductor with `crew up`, `crew rebuild`, `crew restart` or `crew revive`, its one greeting SHALL name each unit of its bolt whose stage is `amended`, and tell it to run construct on each. `crew resume`, which greets nobody, SHALL send the same words as a notice, and only when such a unit exists. When the plan or the bolt's kit cannot be read, the greeting or notice SHALL say so rather than name no unit.

#### Scenario: Restarted with an amended unit
- **WHEN** swb-1's conductor is restarted while a unit of its bolt reads `amended`
- **THEN** its greeting names that unit and tells it to run construct on it, and it does

#### Scenario: Nothing amended
- **WHEN** swb-1's conductor is resumed and no unit of its bolt reads `amended`
- **THEN** it is sent nothing

#### Scenario: A construct already running
- **WHEN** a unit's amended construct has started and not yet committed, and its conductor is restarted
- **THEN** the greeting does not name the unit, whose stage reads `construct`

### Requirement: A landing leaves only what main lacks
Once sluice has landed a bolt's submission to main, crew SHALL mark the bolt's branch as landed on the team's host, and every read of the team SHALL then judge the bolt's work against the kit's main as crew reads it now, never against the bolt: each unit's, chore's and fix's branch, as its place goes, and the bolt's own branch, once no plan has the bolt and no worktree is on it. Whether main holds a commit SHALL be judged by its patch, as `git cherry` compares, never by which commits are ancestors. A branch holding no commit whose patch main lacks SHALL go; one holding such a commit, or one git cannot compare with main, SHALL be kept, named with the commit it is at and how many commits it holds whose patch main lacks, recorded once in the run record, and SHALL then be the user's to keep or delete. `crew bolt land` SHALL print what the tidy it asks of the team's host removed and kept, and once that tidy has run the kit SHALL hold nothing of the bolt's work but branches holding work main lacks and worktrees kept for uncommitted changes or a remove hook.

#### Scenario: Everything the bolt left is on main
- **WHEN** a bolt's submission to main has landed, and `crew bolt land` runs while a merged unit's place and a merged fix's place still stand
- **THEN** both places and their branches are removed, the bolt's branch is removed, and the command says the bolt's branch went because main holds every commit of it

#### Scenario: The bolt's branch holds a commit main lacks
- **WHEN** a bolt's submission to main has landed, and its branch then holds a commit main does not, and `crew bolt land` runs
- **THEN** the bolt's branch is kept, the command naming it, the commit it is at and that it holds 1 commit whose patch main lacks, a run-record entry names the branch, and no later read of the team removes it

#### Scenario: A fix of the landed bolt holds work of its own
- **WHEN** a bolt has landed, and a fix made from it, not merged, holds a commit main does not, and its place goes
- **THEN** its place is removed and its branch is kept and named, compared with main

#### Scenario: The team's host did not answer the landing
- **WHEN** `crew bolt land` runs while the team's host cannot be reached, and the team's next read finds the bolt's branch marked landed and holding a commit main lacks
- **THEN** that read keeps the bolt's branch and names it, as the landing's own tidy would have

#### Scenario: Commits main holds under the same hashes
- **WHEN** sluice landed the bolt as a merge commit whose second parent is the bolt's head, and the tidy compares the bolt's branch and its units' branches with main
- **THEN** `git cherry` finds nothing of their own on them, and they go

### Requirement: A team takes a bolt of any kit it holds
`crew bolt give <team>` SHALL give a team the first bolt, in the plan's order, of any kit it holds that no team holds, or the bolt named when it is of one of them; a bolt of a kit the team does not hold SHALL be refused. While a team holds a bolt, crew SHALL read its stages, make and ready its places, read its policy, make its branch, land it and read its changes in that bolt's kit's checkout on the team's host, and start its conductor and ops there.

#### Scenario: A bolt of the second kit
- **WHEN** slc-1 holds no bolt and the plan's first free bolt among sluice's and weir's is weir's
- **THEN** `crew bolt give slc-1` gives it, reads weir's policy and makes `bolt/<bolt>` in weir on slc-1's host

#### Scenario: A bolt of a kit the team does not hold
- **WHEN** `crew bolt give slc-1 <bolt>` names a bolt of flume
- **THEN** it is refused, naming the kits slc-1 builds

### Requirement: A conductor carries its bolt for sluice
A team's conductor and ops that crew starts while the team holds a bolt SHALL have `SLUICE_BRANCH` in its environment set to the branch of the bolt the team builds, the one given last, and its herdr pane SHALL carry the token `sluice_branch` with the same value, reported from the source `crew` with no time to live. The token SHALL be reported again when herdr resumes the agent, and cleared when the team no longer holds a bolt, set to the other's branch when the bolt it names lands or is dropped while the team holds another; a conductor or ops whose session then carries the old branch SHALL be restarted at its next stop, as an agent an update made due is, told why. A unit slot SHALL carry neither.

#### Scenario: A conductor starts on a bolt
- **WHEN** crew starts `swb-1-conductor` in `/workspace/switchboard-kit/main` while team swb-1 holds bolt `b7`
- **THEN** its environment holds `SLUICE_BRANCH=bolt/b7`, and `herdr pane list` shows `"sluice_branch": "bolt/b7"` in its pane's `tokens`

#### Scenario: A team holding two bolts
- **WHEN** crew starts `swb-1-conductor` while swb-1 holds `b7`, proven and waiting to land, and `b8`, given after it
- **THEN** its environment holds `SLUICE_BRANCH=bolt/b8`, and its pane's token is `bolt/b8`

#### Scenario: herdr restarts
- **WHEN** herdr's server restarts and resumes `swb-1-conductor` without crew's environment
- **THEN** its pane's `tokens` hold `"sluice_branch": "bolt/b7"` again

#### Scenario: The bolt lands
- **WHEN** `crew bolt land b7` closes swb-1's only bolt
- **THEN** the conductor's and ops's panes carry no `sluice_branch` token, and once each is idle it is restarted without `SLUICE_BRANCH`, the conductor fresh and ops with its conversation, told that its team no longer holds `bolt/b7`

#### Scenario: A unit
- **WHEN** crew starts `swb-1-unit-2` in its own worktree
- **THEN** its environment has no `SLUICE_BRANCH` and its pane no `sluice_branch` token

### Requirement: The bolt is forecast against main on the conductor's waits
Each time the conductor waits on a stage through crew, crew SHALL forecast each bolt the team holds against its kit's main and everything queued there, by `sluice submit main --check --head bolt/<bolt> --json` in the kit's main checkout, moving no branch, and the wait SHALL say each forecast once for the same bolt head, revision of main and submissions queued on main it meets, recording it as a `forecast` on the bolt. For a bolt the user left on its kit's landings card or stopped in their words to main-level ops, the wait SHALL say a conflict with what main has landed once as one to take nothing in for while it is left, and SHALL give no take-main fix; once the user ticks it, the wait SHALL give the fix for the same heads as for any bolt.

#### Scenario: Main landed a change to the bolt's lines
- **WHEN** main has landed a change to `shared.txt` that the bolt's own work also changed, and the conductor waits on a unit's stage
- **THEN** the wait says the bolt is forecast to conflict with main on `shared.txt` and gives `crew fix <team> take-main-<main's short sha> "Take main into the bolt: it is forecast to conflict on shared.txt"`, and the forecast is recorded with `Reached: wait`

#### Scenario: Told once
- **WHEN** the conductor waits again and neither the bolt nor main has moved
- **THEN** the wait says nothing of the forecast

#### Scenario: A submission queued on main
- **WHEN** the bolt would conflict on `shared.txt` with `#7` from `design/x`, queued on main ahead of it
- **THEN** the wait says so and that nothing is to be taken in for it, since it may yet fail and leave the queue

#### Scenario: A sluice that can't forecast a branch
- **WHEN** the host's sluice refuses `--head`
- **THEN** the wait says once for the bolt's head that it can't forecast the bolt against main, and why, and goes on

#### Scenario: A bolt the user left

- **THEN** the wait says once that the bolt, which the user left, is forecast to conflict with main on `packages/swancloud-doctor/default.nix`, that nothing is to be taken in while it is left, and that crew tells the take-main fix once they tick it; it gives no `crew fix` command, and the forecast is recorded with `Reached: left`

#### Scenario: Ticked at the same heads
- **WHEN** the user ticks `builds-reach-the-phone` on a later send, neither it nor main having moved, and the conductor waits again
- **THEN** the wait gives the take-main fix for it, unless the card's raise already told it at those heads

### Requirement: A landing is read from GitHub's main
`crew bolt submit` SHALL judge whether a bolt landed by main now, never by sluice's exit alone. For a kit with an origin remote, main now is GitHub's `origin/main` after a fetch. For a kit with no origin, it is the kit's own `main`. Before it submits, crew SHALL read main now on the team's host, and SHALL refuse, submitting nothing, when it cannot. After sluice's outcome it SHALL read main now again, after a fresh fetch, together with whether it holds the bolt's tip that was submitted. The bolt has landed when main now moved and holds that tip, whatever sluice exited. crew SHALL then say where main holds it, mark the bolt's branch landed, and send ops on to `crew bolt land`. When sluice's exit was not 0, crew SHALL also name the exit and sluice's last words as what came after its push, sluice's to report apart from the landing. crew SHALL say that main is where it was only when main now did not move. When main now moved but does not hold the bolt, crew SHALL say the bolt did not land and name where main now is. When main now cannot be read after the submission, crew SHALL say it cannot tell whether the bolt landed, naming why and sluice's exit, and SHALL mark nothing and say nothing of where main is.

#### Scenario: sluice fails after its push
- **WHEN** main-level ops runs `crew bolt submit <bolt>`, and sluice pushes the bolt's merge to GitHub's main, then fails releasing a place and exits 3, leaving the host's own `main` where it was
- **THEN** the command succeeds, saying the bolt landed on the kit's main at the commit GitHub's main holds, naming sluice's exit 3 and its last words as what came after the push, sluice's to report apart, and saying "now crew bolt land <bolt>"; and the bolt's branch is marked landed

#### Scenario: sluice lands it and exits 0
- **WHEN** sluice lands the bolt and exits 0, and GitHub's main holds the bolt's merge after a fetch
- **THEN** crew says the bolt landed at that commit and "now crew bolt land <bolt>", naming no exit, and marks the branch landed

#### Scenario: GitHub's main did not move
- **WHEN** sluice exits 1 or 2, or any other exit, and GitHub's main after a fetch is at the commit it held before the submission
- **THEN** the command fails, saying the bolt did not land and main is where it was, and the branch is not marked

#### Scenario: Main moved, but not with the bolt
- **WHEN** sluice exits non-zero and GitHub's main after a fetch has moved but does not hold the bolt's submitted tip
- **THEN** the command fails, saying the bolt did not land and naming the commit main is now at; it does not say main is where it was, and the branch is not marked

#### Scenario: GitHub can't be read after the submission
- **WHEN** the fetch after sluice's outcome fails
- **THEN** the command fails, saying crew cannot tell whether the bolt landed, with the fetch's words and sluice's exit; it says neither that the bolt landed nor that main is where it was, and the branch is not marked

#### Scenario: GitHub can't be read before the submission
- **WHEN** `crew bolt submit` runs and the fetch of the kit's origin on the team's host fails
- **THEN** it is refused with the fetch's words, nothing is submitted, and no landing worktree is made

#### Scenario: A kit with no origin
- **WHEN** a kit with no origin remote is landed and sluice moves its own `main` to a merge holding the bolt
- **THEN** crew reads the kit's own `main` before and after, and says the bolt landed

### Requirement: A team builds one bolt and may hold one more that waits to land
A team on today's path SHALL build one bolt at a time, and MAY hold one more that is proven and waits to land. A bolt SHALL be proven for this when it holds at least one unit or chore, every one of them has merged, and sluice's verdict at its head is green; a fix in flight on it SHALL NOT make it unproven. The bolt a team builds SHALL be the one it was given last, read from the run record's gives; while it holds one bolt, that one. The proven bolt SHALL keep of the team only what its landing needs: a fix's slot when its landing or a forecast conflicts with main, and the team's ops. The conductor SHALL serve both bolts, and the team SHALL never have more than `units` units, chores and fixes in flight across them. The landing SHALL still run on the user's yes, by main-level ops. When a merge leaves the team free to take a bolt and a bolt of its kits is planned that no team holds, crew SHALL tell the partition's dispatchers that are up, as it does when a bolt lands. A crossed team SHALL hold no such bound: it MAY hold as many bolts as are given it, its room being Flume's on its host.

#### Scenario: The emergency bolt starts
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds bolt `doctor-rows`, whose three units have merged and whose verdict is green at its head while its landing card waits on the user, and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give swc-1`
- **THEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows` and the next planned bolt, the new bolt's branch is made from main, and its units start in swc-1's free slots

#### Scenario: A carrying fix on the waiting bolt
- **WHEN** swc-1 builds `ssh-fix` in three slots and holds `doctor-rows`, whose landing main-level ops says conflicts with main
- **THEN** the conductor starts a fix on `doctor-rows` in the fourth slot, and a fifth piece of work of either bolt waits for a slot

#### Scenario: A merge frees the team
- **WHEN** the last unit of swc-1's only bolt merges, sluice is green at its head, and the plan holds a bolt of swc-1's kit no team holds
- **THEN** each of the partition's dispatchers that is up is told that swc-1 can take its next bolt

#### Scenario: Two bolts, the newer still building
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows`, proven, and `ssh-fix`, given after it, with a unit in code
- **THEN** swc-1 builds `ssh-fix`, and `crew status swc-1` names both bolts, `doctor-rows` as waiting to land

#### Scenario: A crossed team takes a third bolt
- **WHEN** crw-4 is crossed and holds `x` and `y`, each with work in flight, and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give crw-4 z`
- **THEN** crw-4 holds `x`, `y` and `z`, and nothing is refused for the bolts it holds

### Requirement: A close crew refuses is said on the bolt's row and in the run record until it is lifted

### Requirement: A team's path is today's, due or crossed
