## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Teams are fixed in the teams file
crew SHALL read its teams from `~/.config/crew/teams.json` version 2 or version 3, which the machine's configuration writes and crew never edits. Each team names its system, machine, session, its number of unit slots (`units`), and its repos: its kits, one or more, then its partition's blueprints repo, each named once. In version 3 each team SHALL also name `flume`: `false` while it runs today's path, or the name of the Flume stream its work runs in on its host. A version-2 file SHALL be read with every team on today's path, since its schema holds no setting. A team's partition SHALL be the partition of its session in `herdr-hosts.json`. A team with data missing or inconsistent SHALL be an error, never a default.

#### Scenario: A blueprints repo of another partition
- **WHEN** a team in a `clients/willdan` session names afterthought's blueprints as its last repo
- **THEN** crew refuses to load the team, naming the partition's blueprints repos

#### Scenario: The first version of the file
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 1
- **THEN** crew says the file must be regenerated by the machine's configuration, naming versions 2 and 3, and does nothing

#### Scenario: Two teams hold one kit
- **WHEN** slc-1's repos are sluice, weir and blueprints, and flm-1's are flume, weir and blueprints
- **THEN** both load, each holding weir beside its own kit, with no team of weir's own

#### Scenario: Version 3 names each team's setting
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 3, crw-4's `flume` is `"madswan"` and crw-5's is `false`
- **THEN** crew loads crw-4 with its work's stream `madswan` and crw-5 on today's path

#### Scenario: A team in version 3 without its setting
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 3 and crw-6 names no `flume`, or names it `true`
- **THEN** crew refuses to load the file, naming crw-6, the file, and that `flume` is `false` or the name of a stream

#### Scenario: Version 2 still loads
- **WHEN** `teams.json` is version 2, as swancloud writes it before its version-3 deploy
- **THEN** every team loads on today's path

### Requirement: A team builds one bolt and may hold one more that waits to land
A team on today's path SHALL build one bolt at a time, and MAY hold one more that is proven and waits to land. A bolt SHALL be proven for this when it holds at least one unit or chore, every one of them has merged, and sluice's verdict at its head is green; a fix in flight on it SHALL NOT make it unproven. The bolt a team builds SHALL be the one it was given last, read from the run record's gives; while it holds one bolt, that one. The proven bolt SHALL keep of the team only what its landing needs: a fix's slot when its landing or a forecast conflicts with main, and the team's ops. The conductor SHALL serve both bolts, and the team SHALL never have more than `units` units, chores and fixes in flight across them. The landing SHALL still run on the user's yes, by main-level ops. When a merge leaves the team free to take a bolt and a bolt of its kits is planned that no team holds, crew SHALL tell the partition's dispatchers that are up, as it does when a bolt lands. A crossed team SHALL hold no such bound: it MAY hold as many bolts as are given it, its room being Flume's on its host.

#### Scenario: The emergency bolt starts
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds bolt `doctor-rows`, whose three units have merged and whose verdict is green at its head while its landing card waits on the user, and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give swc-1`
- **THEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows` and the next planned bolt, the new bolt's branch is made from main, and its units start in swc-1's free slots

#### Scenario: A carrying fix on the waiting bolt
- **WHEN** swc-1 builds `ssh-fix` in three slots and holds `doctor-rows`, whose landing main-level ops says conflicts with main
- **THEN** the conductor starts a fix on `doctor-rows` in the fourth slot, and a fifth piece of work of either bolt waits for a slot

#### Scenario: A merge frees the team
- **WHEN** the last unit of swc-1's only bolt merges, sluice is green at its head, and the plan holds a bolt of swc-1's kit no team holds
- **THEN** each of the partition's dispatchers that is up is told that swc-1 can take its next bolt

#### Scenario: Two bolts, the newer still building
- **WHEN** swc-1 holds `doctor-rows`, proven, and `ssh-fix`, given after it, with a unit in code
- **THEN** swc-1 builds `ssh-fix`, and `crew status swc-1` names both bolts, `doctor-rows` as waiting to land

#### Scenario: A crossed team takes a third bolt
- **WHEN** crw-4 is crossed and holds `x` and `y`, each with work in flight, and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give crw-4 z`
- **THEN** crw-4 holds `x`, `y` and `z`, and nothing is refused for the bolts it holds

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A team's path is today's, due or crossed
crew SHALL read a team's path from its setting and the bolts the plan gives it, a bolt marked `Path: flume` when it was given while the team was crossed: today's while its setting is `false` and it holds no such bolt; crossed while its setting names a stream and it holds no bolt of today's path; due while the setting and a bolt it holds disagree. A bolt SHALL keep the path it was given on until it lands or is dropped, and nothing SHALL move it or its work to the other.

#### Scenario: The setting turned on while the team builds
- **WHEN** crw-4's setting becomes `"madswan"` while it holds `x`, given on today's path, with a unit in code
- **THEN** crw-4 is due to cross, and `x` goes on with its conductor, ops and slots as it was given

#### Scenario: Empty when the setting turns on
- **WHEN** crw-4's setting is `"madswan"` and it holds no bolt
- **THEN** crw-4 is crossed

#### Scenario: Turned back
- **WHEN** crw-4's setting becomes `false` while it holds `y`, marked `Path: flume`
- **THEN** crw-4 is due back, and `y`'s work goes on through its runner until `y` lands

### Requirement: A team due to cross is given no new bolt
`crew bolt give` SHALL refuse a team that is due, with the plan unchanged and no branch made, naming each bolt it holds and the path it was given on, and saying it is given its next bolt once those have landed or been dropped. crew's tell to the dispatchers that a team can take a bolt SHALL leave a due team out.

#### Scenario: A give to a due team
- **WHEN** crw-4 is due to cross while it builds `x`, and the dispatcher runs `crew bolt give crw-4`
- **THEN** the give is refused naming `x` on today's path, and no branch is made

#### Scenario: A proven bolt waits to land on a due team
- **WHEN** crw-4 is due to cross and holds `x`, proven and waiting to land
- **THEN** no dispatcher is told that crw-4 can take a bolt

### Requirement: A team crosses once its last bolt of today's path has gone
When a team's path reads crossed, its conductor and ops SHALL be due an end, never a restart: crew SHALL end each at its next stop once idle with no question open, or by the hand that gives an idle agent its due restart, never on a held host, closing the team's workspaces and freeing its slots. Once neither is up, crew SHALL record `team.cross` on `team/<team>` naming the stream, and tell the partition's dispatchers that are up that the team takes bolts of its kits whenever one is planned.

#### Scenario: A due team's bolt lands
- **WHEN** crw-4 is due to cross, `x` is the only bolt it holds, and main-level ops lands `x`
- **THEN** crw-4-conductor and crw-4-ops are ended at their next stop, crw-4's workspaces are closed, `team.cross` is recorded for crw-4, and the dispatchers are told

#### Scenario: A held host
- **WHEN** crw-4 has crossed and its host is held
- **THEN** its conductor and ops are not ended until the host goes

### Requirement: A crossed team runs no standing agent
crew SHALL refuse `crew up`, `rebuild`, `restart`, `resume`, `clear` and `down` of a crossed team, and `crew unit run`, `unit wait`, `unit free`, `chore wait` and `fix --wait` on its work, at once and touching no pane, saying that its work runs in Flume places on its host and that `crew bolts` shows them.

#### Scenario: Bringing up a crossed team
- **WHEN** crw-4 is crossed and `crew up crw-4` runs
- **THEN** it is refused naming Flume and `crew bolts`, and no pane is opened

### Requirement: A crossed team's give launches its bolt whole
A give to a crossed team SHALL check the kit's policy and have sluice make `bolt/<bolt>` as any give does, SHALL be refused while the team's host is held or no runner answers for that host, and SHALL NOT be refused for the bolts the team already holds. Its one plan write SHALL set `Team` and `Path: flume` on the bolt and append a launch for each of its units and chores in the file's order, cut from `bolt/<bolt>`. It SHALL start, restart and greet no agent.

#### Scenario: A bolt of three
- **WHEN** `crew bolt give crw-4 x` runs for crossed crw-4, `x` holding units `a` and `b`, `b` after `a`, and chore `c`
- **THEN** the plan marks `x` given to crw-4 with `Path: flume`, `requests.rec` holds three launches of `change`, `change` and `chore` on `bolt/x`, and no agent of crw-4 starts

#### Scenario: No runner on the host
- **WHEN** crw-4 is crossed on deneb and the state repository has no `madswan/runner/deneb`
- **THEN** the give is refused naming that no runner answers on deneb, and no branch is made

### Requirement: A crossed team's launch carries its stream, priority, intent and recipient
Each launch of a crossed team's work SHALL name the team's host and stream, the kit's forge address, the intent `<label>/<bolt>`, the recipient `<label>/planner`, a priority from its bolt's place in the plan, the highest first, and its brief, the work's own words as crew's prompts give them. A write that launches or reorders bolts SHALL append a priority for each launched place whose priority the plan's order changed.

#### Scenario: Two bolts in order
- **WHEN** crossed crw-4 is given `x` and then `y`, `x` above `y` in the plan
- **THEN** `x`'s launches carry a higher priority than `y`'s, each names stream `madswan`, intent `madswan/<bolt>` and recipient `madswan/planner`

#### Scenario: A reorder
- **WHEN** `crew bolt order` puts `y` above `x`
- **THEN** `requests.rec` gains a priority for each launched place of `x` and `y` whose priority changed, and none for another

### Requirement: A crossed team's dependent launches once its After work has landed
A launch of work whose `After` names other work SHALL name those launches, and the runner SHALL launch it only once each of their places has landed on the bolt, answering it held meanwhile, so its place is cut from a bolt that holds their work.

#### Scenario: A dependent held
- **WHEN** crossed crw-4 is given `x`, `b` after `a`
- **THEN** `b`'s launch names `a`'s as its After, and `b` reads waiting on `a` until `a`'s place has landed on `bolt/x`

### Requirement: Plan writes reach a crossed team's places
Each as the write's own act, in its own commit: a unit or chore added to or moved into a crossed team's bolt SHALL be launched as the give launches it; an approved amendment of a unit whose place is launched SHALL be a retry of its construct with the amendment's words, and of one whose launch is held or unanswered a drop of that launch and a new launch carrying the amendment; a unit, chore or bolt dropped SHALL drop each of its launches still standing.

#### Scenario: An amendment of a unit in code
- **WHEN** the user approves an amendment of `a`, whose place on crw-4's runner is in code
- **THEN** `requests.rec` gains a retry of `a`'s construct with the amendment's words, in the approval's commit

#### Scenario: A unit dropped
- **WHEN** the user approves dropping `c`, launched on crossed crw-4's `x`
- **THEN** `requests.rec` gains a drop of `c`'s launch, in the approval's commit

### Requirement: Launched work keeps its bolt and order
A move out of a crossed team's bolt, a split, or a change of `After` on work whose launch is written SHALL be refused, naming its place and drop then add as the way on, since a place's base and order are fixed at its launch.

#### Scenario: A move of launched work
- **WHEN** a proposal moves `a`, launched on crossed crw-4's `x`, to bolt `y`
- **THEN** the proposal is refused naming `a`'s place and that it is dropped and added again

### Requirement: A crossed team's place is answered by a retry
`crew answer <work> "<words>"`, the planner's, the design agent's or main-level ops's, SHALL append a retry of the stage the work's place stopped short on or waits on, carrying the words, and SHALL be refused, naming the place's stage, when the place neither stopped short nor waits on its caller.

#### Scenario: A construct that asked
- **WHEN** the runner's record shows `a`'s construct stopped short asking "Which partition's rail?", and the planner runs `crew answer a "madswan's"`
- **THEN** `requests.rec` gains a retry of `a`'s construct with the words `madswan's`

#### Scenario: Nothing to answer
- **WHEN** `a`'s place is in code with no question, and `crew answer a "yes"` runs
- **THEN** it is refused naming `a`'s stage as code

### Requirement: A crossed team's fix is a fix place on its bolt
`crew fix <team> <name> "<words>"` on a crossed team SHALL append a launch of the fix kind on the bolt's branch, `--bolt` naming the bolt while the team holds more than one, with nothing held; `--drop` SHALL drop it, and `--merge` and `--wait` SHALL be refused naming Flume's submission and `crew bolts`. A fix in flight SHALL hold its bolt's landing.

#### Scenario: A review note marked fix
- **WHEN** the planner runs `crew fix crw-4 rail-word "Say 'land' for a crossed bolt" --bolt x` on a batch's note
- **THEN** `requests.rec` gains a launch of `fix` named `x--rail-word` on `bolt/x`, and `x`'s landing row says it lands once that fix has merged or been dropped

### Requirement: A crossed team's landing that conflicts with main takes main in by a fix place
When sluice refuses a crossed team's bolt at its landing for a conflict with main, the act that reads the refusal SHALL append a launch of a fix on the bolt named `take-main-<main's revision, seven>`, its brief saying to take `origin/main` into the bolt with sluice's words of the conflict, unless a fix of that name already stands; the bolt's row SHALL say it takes main first.

#### Scenario: A landing refused for a conflict
- **WHEN** main-level ops's `crew bolt submit x` reads sluice refusing `x` for a conflict with main at `4d3d9be`
- **THEN** `requests.rec` gains one launch of `fix` named `x--take-main-4d3d9be` with sluice's conflict words, and `x`'s row says it takes main first

#### Scenario: Read twice
- **WHEN** a later read of the same refusal finds `x--take-main-4d3d9be` standing
- **THEN** no second launch is appended
