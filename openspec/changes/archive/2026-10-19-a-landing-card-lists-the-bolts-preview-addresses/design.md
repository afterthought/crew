# Design

## Context

See proposal.md, *Why*. This change is built on `a-landing-card-links-a-session-on-the-bolts-changes`, which comes before it in the bolt: by then `raise_land`'s facts are shared with `crew card session` (`land_facts`), `cards.landing_card` takes the reading's `link` and opens its details with it, a `land/` key is a `READING` kind whose session `row_card_close` ends, and `crew card session` changes the open landing card only while it waits on the user. Where this design names those, it means them as that change builds them.

sluice, as mac-studio runs it (0.29.3, read 2026-10-19 from `sluice preview --help`, `previews --help`, `release --help` and sluice main's `crates/sluice/src/preview.rs`, `openspec/specs/preview/spec.md` and `preview-release/spec.md`):

- `sluice preview <branch> <revision> [--wait] --json`, run in a checkout of the repository, refuses a revision the branch has not landed and a revision whose own `.config/sluice.toml` has no `[preview]` table, each as `{"error": {"kind", "message", "next"}}` on stdout, exit 2. Otherwise it makes the preview's record and checkout with its ports and names, then waits for room in the host's bound with no limit of its own ("waiting: the host is at its bound", a pause loop ended only by Ctrl-C), starts the policy's `[preview] command` and returns at `starting`, or with `--wait` once the command ended, exit 1 if it failed. Its document holds `name` (`preview/<12 hex>`), `branch`, `revision`, `state` (`waiting`, `starting`, `up`, `failed`), `made`, `checkout`, `addresses` ({label: address}, the policy's templates filled in at the making, so known before the stack is up), `names`, `ports`, and `output` when failed.
- One revision has one preview on the host whichever branch asked; asking again gives it back unchanged and starts nothing. sluice never releases a usable preview on its own; a failed one has its stack let go at once but stays listed, with its output, until its caller releases it.
- `sluice previews --json` lists every preview on the host for the repository, each as above. `sluice release <name or revision> --json` releases one, `{"preview", "released"}`, exit 0 also when there was nothing to release.

A preview is the kit's own stack on its fakes; sluice decides nothing by it (sluice `core.19`) and contacts none of its addresses.

Rules this rests on: `plan.16` (the approval of the landing card is the word to land; nothing else is), `briefs.13` (the landing's form, an approval of risk high; one card per thing, changed in place with a change note), `briefs.11` and `briefs.16` (crew raises, changes and closes the card itself, in the conductor's name), `read.1` (what can't be read is said, never guessed), `read.2` (sluice's output read by fields checked against sluice as it is), `read.4` (crew releases and ends only what it asked for and started), `code.2`, `code.7` (each decision a function of facts), `code.9` (a command answers in an agent's shell's time; longer work is handed to a process of its own), `state.4`. Record 0031 §3, amended and accepted 2026-10-20, and §5's landing entry; sluice record 0021. Pages whose areas this touches: `plan.md` (`plan.py`, `kit.py`), `briefs.md` (`conductor.md`), `teams.md` (`plugin/bin/crew`), `tests.md` (`tests/`), `docs.md` (README, the spec).

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** a preview of the bolt's head asked for with the landing card in a kit that declares one, its addresses on the card, a failure said there, the preview replaced when the head moves and released when the card closes, all on the team's host, gating nothing.

**Non-Goals:**
- Making a kit's preview addresses reach the tailnet: the kit's `[preview]` command and addresses are the kit's own (record 0031 §3: swancloud's chore `swanclouds-sluice-policy-declares-a-preview`; how dev names reach the tailnet is swancloud's held dev-URL work).
- A `[preview]` table in crew's own policy: crew's cards show no preview until crew declares one, a decision of its own.
- Any change to the card's approval, its face's list and counts, its reading session, or who lands.

## Decisions

**1. A preview is asked for only where the head's policy declares one.** sluice reads `[preview]` from the previewed revision, so crew does too: `kit_git(…, "show", f"{head}:.config/sluice.toml")` on the team's host, and `kit.previewed(text)` says whether it holds a `[preview]` table (no file, or no table: False; not TOML, or `preview` not a table: refused, naming the file and the head, `read.1`). A refusal there is said on the card as a preview that could not be asked (Decision 4). *Considered:* ask sluice always and read its "no [preview] table" refusal as none: that reads a message's words as a fact (`read.2`), and every kit without previews would ask on every raise.

**2. The ask is handed to a process of its own; the card waits only for the addresses.** `sluice preview` can wait for room on the host without end, and a stack can take minutes, so neither runs inside the command that raises the card (`code.9`), which is the merge's wait or the rail's sweep. `preview_ask(fleet, t, label, bolt, head)`, on the team's host, under a lock on the bolt's record:
- writes crew's record of the ask, `~/.local/state/crew/previews/<label>/<bolt>.json`: `head`, the kit's `main` checkout it runs in, `asked` (UTC), and later the preview's `name`, the handed process's `pid`, what the card was built with (`shown`) and the outcome. It is kept apart from the card's folder, `cards/<label>/land/<bolt>/`, because `end_session` empties that folder whenever the reading session ends;
- hands `crew _preview <label> <bolt> <head>` to its own session (`start_new_session`, its output in `<bolt>.log` beside the record), as `construct_hand` hands a construct; that process records its pid and runs `sluice preview bolt/<bolt> <head> --wait --json` in the kit's main checkout;
- then reads `sluice previews --json` (`sluice_read`) every half second, up to `PREVIEW_START` (20 seconds), for the entry whose `revision` is the head and whose `branch` is `bolt/<bolt>`, its fields checked as `read.2` asks (`name` a string under `preview/`, `addresses` an object of strings, `state` one of the four), and returns `{head, name, addresses, state}`; when the handed process ended first, its JSON error's message or its last line instead, `{head, why}`; when neither came in time, `{head, why: "sluice named no preview within 20 seconds"}`, the handed process left running.

The card is raised with whatever that returns, and never waits longer. *Considered:* `sluice preview` without `--wait` in the raising command, with a timeout: a kill while sluice waits for room leaves the preview `waiting` with nobody to start it, and nothing would read its end to say a failure.

**3. What crew does at each raise is a function of facts.** `preview_step(declared, recorded, head, listed, alive)` → one of `none` (no `[preview]`, nothing recorded), `ask` (none recorded, or the recorded one at this head is neither listed nor its handed process alive), `keep` (recorded at this head, listed or still being asked: read its entry again and ask nothing), `replace` (recorded at another head: release it, then ask), `release` (recorded, and the head's policy no longer declares one). `raise_land` takes the preview through `CardActs.preview(label, key, facts)`, so a logic test hands a stand-in, and puts it in the facts as `preview`; a `keep` gives back the recorded preview's entry as `sluice previews` lists it now. This is what makes the rail's re-raise of a lost card ask nothing new (sluice's own one-preview-per-revision is the second guard), and what replaces the preview when a merge moves the head: the card's change in place for the head (`landing_act("proven")`) carries the new addresses.

**4. The card lists the addresses beside the reading and says what the preview is.** `cards.landing_card` takes `preview` (or None):
- `{head, name, addresses, state}`: the details, after the reading's link and its sentence (the prior change's), say "Try it running: a preview of <head7> sluice brings up, at these addresses once it is up. It lands nothing; approving this card is the word to land." for `waiting` or `starting`, or "…a preview of <head7>, up at these addresses…" for `up`, then one address a line, `<label>: <address>`, in the policy's order as sluice gives it; each address is in the details' links as `Preview: <label>`, and on the card's face as a url artifact of that title after the reading's and before sluice's pull request, as many as Pending You's eight artifacts leave room for (`post_request`'s `artifacts` `maxItems: 8`, read 2026-10-19), the rest in the details alone.
- `{head, why}`, or `failed`: "A preview of <head7> was asked for and is not there: <why>. Nothing waits on it." in the same place, no address listed; for `failed`, `why` is the last lines of sluice's `output`, cut to Pending You's detail budget by whole lines.
- None: the card exactly as without this change.

The title, summary, list of what lands, approval and rationale are unchanged. Each address is listed as the kit's policy names it; crew does not judge whether the phone can reach it (the kit's addresses are reached over the tailnet by record 0031 §3; see *Risks*).

**5. The handed process says the preview's end on the card when it changes what the card says.** When `sluice preview --wait` ends, `crew _preview` writes the outcome (`up`, or `failed` with its output's end, or sluice's refusal) to the record, then decides `preview_change(shown, outcome)` → a change note or None: shown addresses and the outcome failed → "Its preview of <head7> did not come up"; shown a reason (not named in time) and the outcome up → "Its preview of <head7> is up"; shown addresses and up, or shown a reason and failed → None. With a note, and only while the record still names this head (a newer head or a close has moved on) and the card waits on the user (the same reading of its state the prior change gives `crew card session`; factor it as `card_waiting` if it is not one function already), it changes the card in place through `raise_land(…, note=note)`, which rebuilds the facts and takes the preview by `keep`; a card the user has answered is left as it is, since following it with a new one would ask for the landing again. `raise_land` gains `note=None`, used in place of `landing_act("proven")`'s note when given.

**6. Released with the card, by crew's record alone.** `preview_release(label, bolt)`, under the bolt's record's lock: the handed process sent SIGTERM while its pid is alive and `ps` shows it as `crew _preview <label> <bolt>` (`read.4`), then `sluice release <name, or the head when no name was named> --json` in the recorded checkout, and the record removed once sluice answers with its document; a release sluice refuses or that can't run is said and the record kept, so the next close or sweep tries again (`read.1`). It is called:
- by `row_card_close` for a `land/` key, through `CardActs.release_preview(label, key)`, beside the session's end, so `crew bolt land`'s close (`landing_cards_closed`), a fix's withdrawal (`landing_held`) and the sweep's close of a card whose row is gone each release it, whichever way the card closed, saying what was released;
- by `preview_ask` for `replace` and `release`;
- by `crew card sweep --label <label>` on each host, for each record under `previews/<label>/` whose key `land/<bolt>` the rail's judgment holds dead (`judged_dead`) and at least two minutes old by its `asked` (`young`), each acted on printed in the sweep's list as a session is (`{key, unit: null, said, ended}`), so `sweep_sessions` reports it with "its card is closed" or "its row is gone" unchanged.

Only previews crew recorded are released: a preview a person asked for at another revision of the bolt is never touched. One a person asked for at the very head crew then asks for is the same preview, by sluice's rule, and goes with the card.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [A kit's addresses open only on the host, `.localhost` names before swancloud's dev URLs reach the tailnet] → they are listed as the kit names them, and the user's phone can't open them; the kit's `[preview]` is where that is fixed (swancloud's chore), not crew's card. Unlike a plannotator link, whose host crew sets, crew does not refuse them: it can't tell a name the tailnet resolves from one it doesn't.
- [The team's host runs a sluice with no `preview` command, as an older box may] → the handed process ends at once with sluice's usage error, and the card says the preview could not be asked, with that line.
- [A preview holds a stack and ports until released] → it goes with the card; a card that ends with no crew command is caught by the rail's sweep; one whose host is off stays until that host's next sweep, and `sluice previews` names it meanwhile.
- [A preview waiting for room when the bolt lands] → the handed process is ended first, then `sluice release` removes the `waiting` record.
- [The raise waits up to 20 seconds for sluice to name the preview] → the merge's wait and the rail's sweep each take that only when the kit declares a preview and sluice is slow to make its record.

## After deploy

- The first landing card in a kit whose policy declares `[preview]`, swancloud's once its chore lands, opened on the user's phone over the tailnet: each listed address opens the bolt's head running. Its doer the user, on that card, since no check in crew's tiers reaches the phone, the tailnet or a kit's real stack.
