# Design

## Context

Why is in `proposal.md`; the decision is record 0020 (`docs/adr/0020-a-team-is-free-for-its-next-bolt-once-its-current-one-is-proven.md`, accepted 2026-10-18) and the rule `teams.16`.

How crew stands on main today:

- `plan.bolt_give` refuses while `held_by(plan, team)` holds anything, its words from `plan.holding`, which says a unit "has not landed". So a team takes a bolt only once its last one has landed or been dropped, and the give restarts its conductor and ops at once, "since nothing is in flight".
- What "proven" means is already one function: `plan.landing_unready(work, head, verdict, fixes)`, which raises the landing card (record 0031 §1). It reads proven once every unit and chore has merged, not every one landed, `kit.verification(head, verdict)` is green, and no fix is in flight. `teams.16` frees the team on less: every unit merged and the proof green, a fix in flight refusing nothing, since the carrying fix is what the proven bolt keeps of the team.
- The plan already lets a team hold several bolts (`Team` on each bolt; `held_by` returns a list), and most callers loop over them: the forecast (`forecast_orders`, the bolt-against-main forecast), `rebase_dependents`, `amended_afters`, `settle_orders`, `carried_units`, `slot_stage`, `in_bolt`, `amended_units`, the batch-due check and the board's `held(p, team)`. The ones that take the first held bolt are what this change has to settle: `holding` and `bolt_give`, `bolt_held` (the fix's bolt), `held_repo` (the kit `crew.held_team` works in for a team of several kits), `bolt_of_unit`'s fallback, `team_holds` (ops's greeting), `where_of`, `landing_after_merge` and `crew.team_bolt` (the branch a conductor and ops carry for sluice, `teams.20`).
- Slots are per team and never per bolt (`teams.1`), so the cap of `units` in flight holds across two bolts with no change.
- Dispatchers are told by `plan.notify` on a plan write that adds a bolt or frees a team by a landing or a drop. A bolt reading proven is no plan write; it is seen at a merge's end (`landing_after_merge`).

Rules relied on: `teams.16` (built here), `teams.1` (slots bounded across both bolts), `teams.3` (a fix's branch and place name its bolt), `teams.7` (one greeting per start), `teams.19` and `teams.20` (a restart at the next stop when the branch an agent carries changes), `plan.8` (a conflicting landing is taken in by a fix submitted to the bolt; no unit starts on a bolt whose landing is queued), `plan.16` (the landing card and its one procedure), `roles.2` (the landing stays on the user's yes), `read.1` (what can't be read refuses the give), `read.5` (the run record is read from the flywheel's branch), `state.2` (nothing new in the plan records progress), `code.7` (each decision a function of facts), `code.8` (the views and the mod follow the model), `docs.2` (README, the skill and the usage move with `crew fix --bolt`), `docs.4` (specs change through this delta), `docs.6` and `docs.7` (no rule page or record is edited). Pages whose areas the change touches: `plan.md` (`plugin/lib/plan.py`), `teams.md` (`plugin/bin/crew`, `plugin/lib/crew.py`), `briefs.md` (`plugin/roles/`, `plugin/skills/`), `tests.md` (`tests/`), `docs.md` (`README.md`, `openspec/`).

One departure: `docs.4` changes a spec only through a delta, and an OpenSpec delta carries requirements, not a spec's purpose. Record 0020 says the `bolt-teams` purpose reads "builds one bolt at a time and holds at most one more that waits to land" at its next delta, so this change edits that one line of `openspec/specs/bolt-teams/spec.md` beside its delta, in the same build.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A team whose bolt is proven can be given its next one, and a team holding two, or one not proven, cannot.
- Every place that took "the team's bolt" as one bolt takes the right one of two.
- The user sees on the rail and the landing card what waits to land and what a yes frees.

**Non-Goals:**
- The landing itself: card, yes, `crew bolt submit`, `crew bolt land` are unchanged (`roles.2`, `plan.16`).
- A second team per kit, which stays the user's in the teams file.
- Removing `teams.16`'s open item from `_open.md` and its "queued once accepted" clause: the design agent's after the landing (`docs.7`).
- What sluice's board shows of the bolt waiting to land: the conductor's and ops's branch hint names the bolt built (Decision 3); the waiting bolt's queue on sluice's board is not drawn for them.

## Decisions

**1. Proven for a give is its own decision, beside the card's.** `plan.give_refusal(held)`, a function of facts: `held` is each bolt the team holds as `(name, work, head, verdict, unread)`, `work` its units and chores as `(name, kind, stage)` and `unread` why its kit could not be read or None. It returns None, or why the give is refused:
- two bolts held: "<team> holds <a> and <b>; <oldest waiting> waits to land, and its landing frees <team>";
- a kit not read: "can't tell whether bolt <b> is proven: <why>" (`read.1`);
- no work: "bolt <b> holds no units or chores yet";
- a unit or chore not `merged` or `landed` (an `unknown` stage among them): "bolt <b>'s <kind> <name> has not merged (<stage>)";
- `kit.verification` not green: "bolt <b>'s verification is not green: <said>".
A fix in flight refuses nothing, which is where it differs from `landing_unready`; a bolt every unit of which has landed passes too, since only `crew bolt land` is left of it. `holding` goes. *Alternative:* reuse `landing_unready`. Rejected: it holds for a fix in flight and for every unit landed, and `teams.16` holds for neither.

**2. The give reads the kit once, then writes as today.** `bolt_give` reads the held bolts' stages with one `stages_for` (one survey of the team's host, `plan.12`), builds `held` from `st.of` and `st.kit(b)["bolts"][name]`'s `head` and `verdict`, and refuses on `give_refusal`. Inside the write it reads the plan again and refuses when the team now holds two, or a bolt it did not hold when checked ("<team> was given <b> since <tip>"), since the kit's proof was read only for the bolts it held then.

**3. The bolt a team builds is the one given last.** `plan.building(held, gives)`: `held` the bolts' names, `gives` the run record's unrefused `bolt.give` entries on `team/<team>`, oldest first; one held, that one, with no record read; none, None; two, the one whose newest give is later, and a refusal when the record has a give of neither ("can't tell which bolt <team> builds: the run record holds no give of <a> or <b>"). Its reads take `partition_entries` (`read.5`). It replaces the first-held choice in:
- `crew.team_bolt` (through `bolt_of_team`), so `SLUICE_BRANCH`, the pane token, `branch_now` and `hint_standing` name the bolt built. A landing of the waiting bolt leaves the branch where it was, so `teams.20`'s restart is not made due by it; a landing of the built bolt first moves the hint to the other, and is due.
- `held_repo`, so a team of several kits works, by `at_work`, in the built bolt's kit. A command about the waiting bolt in another kit takes the kit of the bolt it names: `fix_cmd` does by `crew.with_kit(t, bolt's Repo)` (Decision 4); a unit's commands already do by `unit_kit_team`.
- `bolt_of_unit`'s fallback, used only when a branch has no `bolt/` upstream.
- `team_holds`, so ops's greeting names the bolt built and the one waiting, when there are two.
- `where_of` names every bolt the team holds.
*Alternative:* the bolt not proven. Rejected: it needs a kit read in `team_bolt`, every start, and two proven bolts leave it unanswered. *Alternative:* the plan's order. Rejected: a named give or `crew bolt order` can put the newer first.

**4. A fix names its bolt while two are held.** `crew fix <team> <name> "<words>" [--bolt <bolt>]`, and `--bolt` after `--merge`, `--drop` and `--wait` too. `fix_cmd` takes the bolt by `plan.fix_bolt(held, named, slots, name)`, a function of facts: the bolt named, refused when the team does not hold it; else the slot's fix by name among `fix/<b>/<name>` for each held bolt, refused when two match; else, starting, the one held, refused when two are held ("<team> holds <a> and <b>: name the fix's bolt with --bolt"). `fix_in_slot` keeps its shape and is called with the chosen bolt. `bolt_held` goes. The forecast's take-main words (`plan.py` near "Take main into the bolt now") add `--bolt <bolt>` when the team holds two bolts, so the conductor's copied command goes to the bolt forecast. `t` is `crew.with_kit(t, the bolt's Repo)` so a fix of the waiting bolt in another kit is made in that kit.

**5. A give beside a waiting bolt restarts at the next stop.** When the team held a bolt before the give, `bolt_give` restarts nothing: it hands its conductor and ops that are up to `hold.hand` (`crew _renew`), as `team_tidy` does when the branch changes (`teams.20`), since `branch_due` now finds the branch they carry is not the bolt built. The renewal's conductor is fresh, as every conductor restart is, so its greeting must carry what it held in its head: `greet_team`'s conductor line, for a team holding two bolts, says "Your team now builds the bolt <new>. Bolt <old> waits to land", then, when the conductor holds an open landing card for it on this host (`landing_card_keys` and `cards.card_under` on `cards.host_entries`), "its landing card <key> is yours: call get_request (requestId <id>, name <team>-conductor), then run crew card wait <key> in the background", then each fix in flight on it from the kit read (`fixes_in_flight`) with "wait on it with crew fix <team> <name> --wait --bolt <old>". The renewal greets through the same `greet_team`. A give to a team that held none keeps today's immediate `crew restart --no-greet` and greeting. *Alternative:* restart at once as today. Rejected: a conductor waiting on its landing card or a carrying fix would lose that wait mid-way, where `renew_ready` waits for it to be idle with only crew's waits running.

**6. A merge's end raises the landing of the bolt it merged into, and tells the dispatchers when the team is free.** `landing_after_merge(fleet, t, bolt)` takes the bolt the merge went into (the wait knows it: `o.obj`'s bolt, or `bolt_of_unit`), and raises that bolt's landing card. When that bolt now reads proven for a give and the team holds another bolt that is proven too, the other's card is changed through `raise_land` with the note "a yes now frees <team> for <next>" (Decision 7). Then, when `give_refusal` over the team's held bolts is None and `to_give` finds a planned bolt for the team, crew tells each of the partition's dispatchers that are up, as `notify` does: "<team> can take its next bolt: bolt <b> reads proven. Give a free team its next bolt." The read of whether to tell is a function, `plan.free_now(refusal, planned)`. A tell that comes again on a later merge while the dispatcher has not given is harmless: its give is refused or takes the planned bolt.

**7. What a yes frees is one fact the row and the card share.** `plan.yes_frees(this, others, planned)`: `this` the bolt the row is of, `others` the team's other held bolts as `(name, proven)`, `planned` the bolt `to_give` would give the team or None. With no other bolt, None: the team is free already. With another not proven: `("builds", <it>)`. With another proven: `("frees", planned)`, `planned` None saying nothing is planned for the team. `rail_rows`' land row's first line becomes "bolt <b> (<l>), <team> on <host>: every unit merged; waits to land; <l>-ops lands it", followed, by `yes_frees`, by "<team> builds <c> meanwhile", "a yes frees <team> for <d>" or "a yes frees <team>, and nothing is planned for it". `land_facts` puts the same into the card's facts (`frees`), and `cards.landing_card` adds to its summary, after the line on main, "A yes frees <team> for <d>, which waits behind this landing." or "A yes frees <team>, and nothing is planned for it." and nothing for `builds` or None, since the card asks only for the landing. The proven read inside the row is the row's own (every unit merged, `kit.verification` green), as `give_refusal` reads it.

**8. The board and the bands draw a waiting bolt.** `Stages.bolt_state` returns `"waits"` once every unit and chore has merged, not all landed, and `kit.verification` is green at the head read; `board.bolt_row` draws it as "waits to land", where it draws a state other than active today. `conductor_view` and `team_rows` already draw every held bolt. `plugin/hooks/status.js` draws the segments it is given and reads no state, so it does not change; `tests/py/test_board.py`'s case pinning every key the mod reads stays as it is (`code.8`).

**9. A landing says what the team still holds.** `bolt_land`'s closing line becomes "<bolt> has landed at <kit>'s <sha>; <team> builds <other>" while the team holds another, and "… and <team> holds no bolt" otherwise.

**10. The briefs and the docs.** The conductor's brief: its opening says the team builds one bolt at a time and may hold one more, proven, that waits to land; that it serves both, the waiting bolt keeping only its landing card and a fix when its landing or a forecast conflicts; and *Fixes* says to name `--bolt` while two are held, a carrying fix's being the waiting bolt, and to start a fix on the waiting bolt in the first slot that frees, ahead of the new bolt's next stage, since it holds the landing. Ops's brief: its opening says the same of what the team holds. The dispatcher's brief, *Giving bolts*: a team builds one bolt and may hold one more that waits to land; give its next once crew tells it the team's bolt reads proven, or its bolt has landed, or it holds none; crew refuses the give while a held bolt is not proven or the team holds two, so a refusal is no fault, and it waits for the next tell. README and `plugin/skills/crew/SKILL.md` say a team builds one bolt and may hold one more that waits to land, and `crew fix … --bolt`; `plugin/bin/crew`'s usage gives `--bolt` on each `crew fix` line (`docs.2`).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [A conductor restarted fresh after a give forgets the waiting bolt's state beyond what the greeting says] → The greeting names the bolt, its open landing card with the adopting line, and each fix in flight; the rest the conductor reads from `crew bolts` and `crew status` as its brief says.
- [Two bolts' work competes for slots: a carrying fix for the old bolt waits behind the new bolt's stages] → Record 0020 accepts it ("a carrying fix for the old competes for slots with the new bolt's stages; the conductor's brief has to say both"); the conductor's brief says a fix on the waiting bolt goes first when a slot frees, since it holds the landing.
- [The run record holds no give of a held bolt, for a bolt given before gives were recorded, beside one given after] → Only a team holding two bolts reads the record, and that happens only after this lands, each give since recorded by `bolt_give`. When it does happen, `building` refuses and names it; `team_bolt` prints that and starts the role in the main checkout with no branch, as it does for an unreadable plan today.
- [Sluice's board, read once per session, names only the bolt built] → The waiting bolt's queue is ops's landing and main-level ops's, and `crew bolts` shows its verification.
- [A tell to the dispatchers at every merge's end while the team is free] → It is sent only when a planned bolt waits for the team, and a give takes it at once.

## Migration Plan

Nothing is migrated: no plan field is added (`state.2`), and every team holds at most one bolt when this lands.

## After deploy

None. Every scenario is proved by the logic and end-to-end tiers on stub herdr and sluice, and the restart of the briefs' agents after the landing is crew's own (`teams.19`).
