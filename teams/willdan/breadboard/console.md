`node-bun/apps/brd-console` is the console mockup the real console grows out of. The tasks and the book say what must be true; the mockup shows how it should look and feel. Before building a task that touches the console, open the mockup screen it covers and build to it: layout, spacing, type, components, and what happens on a click or a key. Where the user or the book describes something differently from the mockup, that description wins.

Before committing console work, screenshot the console your build serves and the same mockup screen with headless Chrome (`"/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" --headless=new --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars --window-size=1440,900 --screenshot=<file> <url>`), look at both, and fix what differs. Put the screenshot paths in your summary so the user can look too.
