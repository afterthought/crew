## Context and problem statement

wldn's swb-2 was given its bolt at 23:48Z on 2026-10-26, one unit of three tasks. Its construct delivered at 00:16Z. The conductor's brief says code starts at once on a unit whose change is committed, with nobody asked; the conductor started nothing, recorded nothing, and answered none of the six tells ops and the planner sent it between 12:29Z and 13:06Z. It moved at 18:09Z, on a person's word in its pane; code ran at 18:25Z. Seventeen hours of a bolt's eighteen and a half went to a team that held startable work and did nothing, and nothing in crew noticed: `teams.4` watches a stage's agent for quiet, not the conductor, and the conductor is the one thing that starts a stage. In the same partition swb-3's bolt sat a day because its landing's close was refused and said on stderr alone (record 0040's unit of 2026-10-27, `work joins a bolt only while it builds`, says it on the row), and swb-4's has waited a day on a card of the user's own. The user's aim is not a short bolt: a bolt agents code for hours is welcome. It is that no team sits idle with work it could do, and that nothing slows a bolt at its gates.

Where a bolt's time goes is three parties' and crew should show which. The team's: its stages, its fix rounds, its merges through the bolt's gate, as long as the work takes. The kit's: the checks its policy runs at each merge and at the landing; a Switchboard landing's gate runs fifteen to forty-five minutes today, on `root:integration`, which is the kit's to shorten, and crew's part at a gate is to hand the bolt on the moment the gate answers, a landing's close said on the row when it is refused. The user's: every card that waits on them, a batch's notes, a landing's send, a conductor's question. crew owns the fourth, which should be nothing: the time a bolt is held by nobody.

Flume's callers table leaves plans, bolts, slots, the dispatcher and conductors to crew, so a bolt's clock and a stalled team are crew's to rule; what a team is bound to at once stays Flume's (record 0043's thread). Under Flume a kind's stages run in order by Flume's own act (`change@1`: construct, code, verify), the caller deciding only how many places run; a next stage that starts itself is that shape, built in crew now and met by Flume's run once crew's kits run under it.

## Decision outcome

1. **The next stage starts by crew's own act.** Once a stage delivers, crew starts the one that follows it as soon as its gate holds, on the team's host, by the act that read the delivery, and tells the conductor what started: a construct whose change is committed is followed by code; a code stage whose every task is ticked, or that delivers by its commits, by verify; a verify delivered, by the merge, once every unit or chore it comes after has merged; a chore's code once what it comes after has something committed. A start crew refuses is said on the bolt's row with the refusal, and the conductor decides it: a fix round, a conflict, the user's words. The conductor is told, never waited on; `plan.15`'s construct on an approved amendment is this rule's first case.
2. **A stalled team is raised and renewed, and asks the user nothing.** A team whose bolt holds startable work with no stage running, no submission of its queued and no card of its own open is stalled once five minutes pass: crew tells its conductor what is startable and the partition's operator that the team stalled; a conductor that has not moved five minutes later is renewed by its host's dispatcher, its conversation kept, and told; the stall is on the bolt's row and in the run record. No card asks the user about a stall, and no agent waits on the user for it.
3. **The row shows a bolt's age and who holds it.** A bolt's row, on the rail and on the board, shows its age since its give and where the time went by holder: the team, a stage running or a submission in a gate; the kit, a gate's checks running; the user, a card of the bolt's open; another bolt's landing it waits on; nobody, stalled. Its landing's entry records the split, so an idle team or a slow gate is read in minutes against each holder's share and the user sees whose minutes they were.
4. **Landed is read, never remembered.** A bolt whose branch sluice's record shows landed on main is recorded landed by the first act of crew's that reads the bolt after it, on any host, the wait that saw it or another command's read, and `crew bolt land` by hand is never the only way; a close crew refuses is said on the bolt's row and in the run record until it is lifted, as record 0040's unit of 2026-10-27 has it, and a bolt sluice has landed is never held a day for want of the command.

## Considered options

1. The next stage starts itself, a stalled team is raised and renewed by the machine, the row shows the split (chosen): nothing of a bolt's cycle rests on an agent remembering, and the user is asked nothing a renew can settle.
2. Raise an idle team to the conductor and the operator after fifteen minutes, and show time since the last stage on the rail (wldn-planner's remedy). Taken in part: the stall is raised, but to a conductor that already failed to act, so the dispatcher renews it; a stall is a failure of crew's own, so its bound is five minutes, not fifteen; and the age alone does not say whose the minutes were.
3. A card to the user when a team stalls. Rejected: the user is asked about a failure of crew's own, which they have refused before (record 0037's thread, 2026-10-23), and a card adds their latency to the bolt's.
4. A bolt sized to its cycle, thirty minutes from give to landed, as the user's first words read. Rejected by the user's correction: a bolt coded for hours is wanted; what is not is an idle team or a slow gate.
5. The stall and the row's clock as Flume's, with the team's bound and the kinds. Rejected: Flume's callers table keeps bolts, conductors and the dispatcher crew's; the stall and the row are crew's to show and keep.
6. A bolt blocked on the outside world, an allowance or the user's hand, parked, its slots freed and the team given its next bolt, resumed when the wait ends (wldn-design's ask, swb-4's case). Held, not decided here: whether a team holds one bolt or more at once is the team's bound, which is Flume's and is on the user's card after record 0043 (`ask/swancloud-design/crews-work-under-flume-2026-10-27`); it is decided there, and this record neither parks nor forbids parking.

## Consequences

Four crew units once accepted: the next stage's start by crew's act; the stall watch with the conductor's renew; the row's age and split; a landing recorded by whatever reads it. What this record does not shorten: a kit's own gate (Switchboard's `root:integration`, wldn's to read) and the user's own cards.
