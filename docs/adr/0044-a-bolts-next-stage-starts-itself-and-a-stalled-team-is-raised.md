## Decision outcome

1. **No work is held by memory.** A conductor that holds a unit for an event, another bolt's landing, a card's answer, a reading someone owes, holds it through a wait of crew's that ends at the event and says so, never by a note to itself; crew records the hold on the bolt's row, what the unit waits for and since when, and ends it when the event comes, telling the conductor what is now startable. A hold crew has no wait for is not a hold: the unit is startable, and its conductor starts it.
2. **Another bolt's landing is never what a unit waits for.** A bolt ships alone (record 0042, rejected by the user on 2026-10-26): a unit whose construct finds its work overlapping what another bolt will land is built on its own bolt as written, and the bolt takes main in when sluice's forecast says it must (`teams.21`, `plan.8`); the planner proposes no hold on another bolt's landing, and crew refuses a wait on one.
3. **A team holding startable work with nothing running is said.** A team whose bolt holds startable work with no stage running, no submission of its queued, no card of its own open and no hold crew records, five minutes on, is stalled: crew says so on the bolt's row and tells its conductor what is startable and the partition's operator that the team stalled, and the run record holds it. No card asks the user about it.
4. **Landed is read, never remembered.** A bolt whose branch sluice's record shows landed on main is recorded landed by the first act of crew's that reads the bolt after it, on any host, and `crew bolt land` by hand is never the only way; a close crew refuses is said on the bolt's row and in the run record until it is lifted, as record 0040's unit of 2026-10-27 has it.
5. **The row shows a bolt's age and who holds it.** A bolt's row, on the rail and on the board, shows its age since its give and where the time went by holder: the team, a stage running or a submission in a gate; the kit, a gate's checks running; the user, a card of the bolt's open; a hold, what for and since when; nobody, stalled. Its landing's entry records the split, so an idle team, a hold or a slow gate is read in minutes against each holder's share.

## Considered options

1. A hold is a crew wait on the row, a stall is said, landed is read, the row shows the split (chosen): what swb-2 lacked was a wait and a notice, and what swb-3's earlier bolt lacked was a read; each is the machine's, and the user is asked nothing.
2. crew starts every next stage itself and renews a stalled conductor (this record's first version). Rejected by the user and by the logs: swb-2's conductor was awake and holding on the planner's word, so a renew would have brought back the same hold, and a stage started over a deliberate hold takes the conductor's judgement away.
3. Raise an idle team to the conductor and the operator after fifteen minutes, with time since the last stage on the rail (wldn-planner's remedy). Taken in part, as the stall and the row's split; a hold with a recorded wait is not idle, which the age alone could not tell.
4. A card to the user when a team stalls. Rejected: a failure of crew's own, which the user has refused before, and a card adds their latency.
5. A bolt sized to its cycle, thirty minutes from give to landed, as the user's first words read. Rejected by the user's correction: a bolt coded for hours is wanted.
6. A bolt blocked on the outside world parked, its slots freed and the team given its next bolt (wldn-design's ask, swb-4's case). Held: what a team holds at once is the team's bound, Flume's, on the user's card after record 0043; this record neither parks nor forbids it.

## Consequences

Four units, each built where the function belongs, as the user's note on accepting has it, not added to crew: a hold that waits on an event and ends at it, and a place with runnable work that nobody runs, are Flume's, which runs stages and their waits (its requirements' callers table and `change@1`), and crew's only until its kits run under Flume; a landing known the moment sluice records it is sluice's to tell and crew's to read, never a hand command; the row's age and split is crew's rail and board. The planner proposes each with the design agent of the tool it belongs to, madswan-design for Flume and sluice, and proposes none into crew that Flume or sluice should carry. The conductor's brief says a hold is a crew wait and that no unit waits for another bolt's landing; the planner's brief says it proposes no such hold. What this record does not shorten: a kit's own gate (Switchboard's `root:integration`, wldn's to read) and the user's own cards.
