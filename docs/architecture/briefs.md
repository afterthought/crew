# Brief rules

The rules every role's brief holds to: what an agent may do, must never do, and how it speaks to the user. `core.md` binds here too, and `roles.1` to `roles.4` there bind every brief. Why rulings cite readings is record 0004; why decisions are shown whole is 0002.

## Rules

- **[briefs.1]** Each role stays in its job: the planner never starts units or drives a team; the design agent never puts a unit into a bolt; a dispatcher never changes a bolt's content; the operator agent writes nothing but the teams file, on the user's word; the reviewer writes nothing but its batch's summary file; the conductor writes no design, specs or code; ops edits no code, deploys nothing and proves nothing, a bolt's proof being its verification and a deploy the kit's deployer's (`plan.14`), and main-level ops edits only main.
- **[briefs.2]** A stage agent writes only within its scope and never pushes: construct commits only under its change; a coder treats the change as frozen, ticking tasks in the commit that does them, but for its Touches block, which a code stage corrects on a verify finding the report marks fix, and its archive, which a unit's merge stage makes before it merges (record 0006); a fix's coder touches nothing under `openspec/`; verify changes nothing.
- **[briefs.3]** A crew brief says nothing of what a kit's change holds beyond what OpenSpec's own schema and the kit's `openspec/config.yaml` say, which the stage's command hands the agent; crew reads of a change only what its machinery needs, its name, its tasks' boxes, its proposal's Touches block and its archive at merge; no brief names a proof list, a task's shape or a design's sections, and ops works no list from a change.
- **[briefs.4]** An agent that puts a decision before the user shows it whole, a proposal as `crew plan proposed <n>` prints it and constructed changes as a batch card with each unit's summary and its whole change, asks for the answer in words, and never recites an answering command; the conductor opens nothing for a batch.
- **[briefs.5]** A ruling names the documentation or reading it rests on, is made after reading the thing itself, rules the outcome and constraints rather than the sequence, and answers linked questions together; an intent names the outcome and the records that govern it, never the mechanism; corrections to a unit under construction are batched until its construct settles.
- **[briefs.6]** An agent that stops to wait on the user tells each of the partition's operator agents in one line what it waits for and where, and never asks the user to do what it can do itself.
- **[briefs.7]** Test, check and proof results reach the user as one bullet per suite or proof before any prose: ✅ passed, ❌ failed with the count and a one-line cause, ⏳ not yet run.
- **[briefs.8]** Without the user's word, no agent acts on an agent that is working, or clears, restarts or ends a slot's stage agent; every brief speaks plain English and describes what the user sees and does.
- **[briefs.9]** A card that asks the user to read something it cannot hold whole carries the link to a plannotator session crew starts on that document without opening a browser on the host, in the card's own words first and among its links, never only as an attached artifact: a batch's one reading session, on the batch's folder holding the reviewer's summary whole and every unit's whole change as one review, a landing's one reading session on the bolt's changes against main, every unit's and chore's as one review, offered beside the approval and gating nothing (Proposed 2026-10-19, record 0031 amendment, on the user's card; accepted only on their yes.), and a proposal of more than fifteen new units; every other card holds what the user decides from whole, as prose that wraps in short paragraphs with nothing depending on Markdown rendering, the detail folded under the decision; the session's annotations are read as the card's notes, and the session ends when the card closes.
- **[briefs.11]** crew raises the rail's cards itself, each at its point in the process and in the name of the role whose row it is, the planner's the proposals' and the batches', a conductor's its bolt's landing, main-level ops's each deploy offered for the user's word and each after-deploy step that is the user's or held for their word (`plan.14`), the design agent's its records'; no agent builds, posts or closes a row's card. A question for the user is asked by a conductor, the planner, main-level ops or the design agent, in its own name, through Pending You's own tools in its session, once the chain of command has not answered it: a stage agent and a team's ops stop short to their conductor (`crew needs`), the conductor asks the design agent, main-level ops or the planner before the user, the dispatcher asks through the planner, and the operator agent asks nothing, the user being in its pane. crew records every such card on the rail, refuses a card from any other role and a crew agent's own post, change or close under a row's key, withdraws a question whose asker's session has ended, and refuses nothing else by ruling but a call in another agent's name (`briefs.17`).
- **[briefs.12]** Constructed and amended changes reach the user in batches that span the partition, due every N changes across its teams or T hours, whichever comes first, summarized by the main level's reviewer and posted by the planner as one card that says what it is, names every unit and links one reading session on the batch; the planner sends each unit's notes from the session and the card to its team's conductor, which runs construct again with them, and the unit goes on with no approval asked; notes on a merged unit are a fix or new work; and a bolt is offered for landing only once every unit's latest change has been in a batch whose card the user has answered.
- **[briefs.13]** Each of crew's six cards has one form, built by crew from the event's facts and never by an agent: a record's an approval of low risk, reversible, holding the record whole; a proposal's an approval with the change drawn and in text and a choice per new unit or chore to leave it out; a batch's one notice for the whole batch, never split, saying first what it is, naming every unit with its team, asking nothing and closed by one tap or by its reading session's send, its notes the session's annotations and the card's own note, both named on it; a landing's and a deploy's an approval of risk high, not reversible, with why, a landing's linking one reading session on the bolt's changes against main, offered and never required (Proposed 2026-10-19, record 0031 amendment, on the user's card; accepted only on their yes.); a step's a `do` with the step's words, where, and each command whole when the user is its doer, and a choice between now, when the host is quiet and skipping when an agent is; one card per thing, changed in place with a change note when its thing changes, never a second card under a new key; every card's words prose that wraps with nothing depending on Markdown rendering, never a required words question and never a command for the user to run that an agent can run on their yes; a question's form is its asker's. A step's card is changed in place when the step is recorded again with other words, another doer or another hold while it is open, and withdrawn and posted again following itself when the doer moves between the user and an agent, since Pending You changes no card's kind; and a card that asks questions, a proposal's whose rows fill more than one list, is never changed in place: while it waits it is withdrawn with the change note and its new version posted following it, since Pending You changes no card's questions, and so is a proposal's card that a rewrite moves to another form, a hold-to-approve, a choice, one list or lists, since it changes no card's kind.
- **[briefs.14]** A unit's sources govern its how: a stage reads them from the kit's main as they stand when it starts, and where the intent says more than its sources or otherwise, the stage builds from the sources and names the difference first in its final message.
- **[briefs.15]** A decision record the design agent writes or amends is committed as proposed and becomes accepted only on the user's yes, recorded in it with their words: given in the pane where the record was settled with them, or on the one card crew raises for it in the record's own words, through `crew record`, when it was not; what the user settled in their own words in the design talk is recorded as their decision, and the record says so.
- **[briefs.16]** crew posts, changes, closes and reads its own six cards itself, as the asking assistant through the fleet's `pendingyou mcp` under the host's sign-in, in the name of the role whose row it is and under the row's key, filled out at its end to Pending You's eight characters when it is shorter, recording each act under the row's key from Pending You's own reply; a crew agent's call to a Pending You tool that posts, changes or closes a card is refused under a row's key, filled out or not, and from any role but the four that may ask (`briefs.11`), and otherwise allowed, but in another agent's name (`briefs.17`), and recorded; crew and every session share the host's one sign-in, `pendingyou-signin`, and no account folder holds a Pending You login of its own.
- **[briefs.17]** A crew agent's call to any Pending You tool names its caller only as the agent itself, and crew refuses it before it runs otherwise, in words naming the agent's own name and saying the call would reroute another agent's answers to this session: every `name` but an area's, whoami's `previousName`, match_area's `task` and post_request's `session.label`; a call that names no caller passes, since it claims no agent's name and its session keeps the one its last named call gave; the check stands apart from which role may post and under which key (`briefs.11`, `briefs.16`), and outlasts any change to those.
- **[briefs.10]** Anything sent to another team or partition is sent with `crew send <team or partition> "<text>"`, which delivers it to that partition's operator agent, on the sender's host when it has one there, else on the partition's main-level host, and records the send; every brief says so in one line, names nothing else to send to, and no agent sends a team's or a partition's business to its conductors.
