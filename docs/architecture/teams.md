# Team rules

The rules for teams, slots, stages, panes and tells: the bash command, the role launcher and the builder. `core.md` binds here too; the how-to is README's Teams section and `plugin/skills/crew/SKILL.md`. When a stage ends is record 0007.

## Rules

- **[teams.1]** A slot is freed, and its place removed, once its unit, chore or fix has merged, landed, been dropped or left the team's bolt, and its agent has stopped; a team never has more than `units` in flight.
- **[teams.2]** Every unit stage starts as a fresh agent in its slot after the slot's previous agent is ended; crew never starts a role over an agent still running, `stop` fails rather than lies when the agent did not exit, and a working agent is ended, cleared or freed only with `--force`.
- **[teams.3]** A fix is built on `fix/<bolt>/<name>` at `<kit>/places/fix-<bolt>--<name>` from its team's bolt; a fix whose branch or place already exists is refused, and a fix's agent starts or merges only in a worktree on its own branch tracking the bolt.
- **[teams.4]** A stage ends when its deliverable exists in the kit or on the team's host, or when its agent stops short and says what it needs, never when its pane goes quiet; whoever waits learns the end with the deliverable, and a stage quiet past crew's limit is reported stuck.
- **[teams.5]** Code is refused until a unit's change is committed, never for want of a review; verify until every task is ticked, or for a chore until its place holds a commit; merge until every unit or chore it comes after has merged, and a chore's until verify has run since its code and its every commit carries its trailer; and a unit, chore or fix merges into its bolt only through `wt merge bolt/<bolt> --no-squash --no-remove`, or, where the kit opts into sluice by a flag in its config, `wt submit bolt/<bolt>`, whose verdict replaces the merge hooks' suite gate; a fix whose branch is the bolt's own commits replayed on a commit the kit's main holds, as many as the bolt's and in order each the same commit by message and author, equal or changed, becomes the bolt's head by crew's move, never by a merge or a submission.
- **[teams.6]** A role's model and effort come only from its definition's frontmatter, overridden only under `roles.<role>` in the teams file; `crew-role` starts every role with `CREW_AGENT`, `CREW_LABEL` and `SLUICE_AGENT` (the agent's name again, which sluice reads) set and the filled brief appended to the system prompt, in the role's folder, and `crew resume` picks up the agent's last session in the folder it began in.
- **[teams.7]** A conductor, and every agent crew starts outside a team (a main level's design agent, planner and ops, a dispatcher, an operator agent), gets exactly one greeting per start, marked as crew's; a plan write whose own command greets a team does not also notify its conductor.
- **[teams.12]** A unit's Touches is a fenced list of paths or globs under `## Touches` in its proposal, read by crew before code; an overlap with an unmerged unit ahead of it in the plan, and a conflict `git merge-tree` forecasts on the conductor's waits, reach the later unit once, told to its agent when idle, in its next stage's prompt when none runs, or as a settle stage when it waits to merge; a forecast between two unmerged units gives the later `After` on the earlier; overlap orders only merging, never construct, code or verify; a path the unit's own commits changed and it did not declare is a verify finding, never a path of another unit's work its place took.
- **[teams.13]** A conflict between two units, forecast, met at a rebase or met at merge, is resolved in the later unit's place by its agent, handed the other unit's change and the conflicting paths, keeping both intents; the earlier unit is not changed for it.
- **[teams.14]** A settle stage starts, fresh, in the slot of a unit that has delivered its verify and waits to merge, when a forecast or an amended `After` unit reaches it; its deliverable is a commit on the unit's branch made since it began, or its agent stopping short to say nothing needs to change, and the unit then merges.
- **[teams.15]** A stage's prompt, and a greeting's, is taken or sent again: crew sends it only once the pane's agent accepts input, reads that the agent's transcript has it within a short time, sends it once more when it has not, and reports the stage as never started when the second sending is not taken either; text left typed in a pane for a hand to submit is never a stage's state.
- **[teams.16]** A team builds one bolt at a time and holds at most one more that is proven and waiting to land, every unit merged and its proof green: from that moment the dispatcher may give the team its next bolt, and crew refuses a give only while the held bolt has an unmerged unit or a proof not yet green; the proven bolt keeps of the team only what its landing needs, a carrying fix's slot and its ops, and the landing still runs on the user's yes. (Proposed 2026-10-11, record 0020, on the user's card; accepted only on their yes.)
- **[teams.11]** A tell reaches its agent as a prompt or not at all: an agent that is not up, or waiting on a question in its pane, is reported and the command fails with nothing typed.
- **[teams.8]** A pane crew starts answers only Claude's folder-trust question, never the Bypass Permissions warning.
- **[teams.9]** The panes, layout and slots files change only by writing a new file and moving it over the old; the stages file changes only under `flock`; output that bash evals is shell assignments quoted with `shlex.quote`, and everything else such a command says goes to standard error.
- **[teams.10]** A brief fills every `{{TOKEN}}` from the builder, a token with no builder or no data is a refusal, never blank text, and every brief carries the roster with how to read crew's marks.

## Details

**[teams.1]** Rules out: a slot kept for a requeued unit; a fifth unit on a four-slot team; a chore's slot kept once its trailer is on the bolt (`plan.11`, `plan.py` `slot_stage`). Not ruled out: the partition's reviewer, a main-level role that takes no slot (ADR 0014). Source: fixes e413c48, f6988a2; `bolt-teams` spec "Units run in slots"; worktrees-go-with-their-work; `crew.py` `slot_for`, `reaped`; `plan.py` `reap`; `tests/py/test_slots.py`.

**[teams.2]** Rules out: a launch typed into a running Claude; `stop` reporting success on a live agent. Source: fixes 499cd3b, f6988a2; `crew.py` `Team.stop` and `Team.start` for a slot's agent, `plugin/bin/crew` `stop` and `start` for a standing role's; `tests/py/test_slots.py`.

**[teams.3]** Rules out: `fix/<name>` with no bolt; a merge from another place; adopting an existing place. Source: fixes 5e11de2, f6d7b11, 4fd29d2; fix-place-names-its-bolt.

**[teams.4]** Rules out: `stage.end` at herdr's first idle with no deliverable; a conductor reading a pane for an outcome; a two-hour wait on a stage already done. Source: ADR 0007; `a-stage-ends-at-its-deliverable`; `plan.py` `deliverable`, `stage_wait` (`_wait`) and its `look`, `stage_needs` (`_needs`) and `stage_ends`; `transcript.py` `pending`; `tests/py/test_stage_ends.py`, `tests/py/test_wait.py`, `tests/t-journey-units.sh`, `tests/t-journey-merge.sh`.

**[teams.5]** Rules out: `--no-hooks`, `--yes`, a squash; code held for the user's review; a dependent merged ahead of its dependency; `wt submit` for a kit that has not opted into sluice; a chore merged with a commit that lacks its `Chore:` trailer, which would never read merged (`plan.11`, `plan.py` `chore_refusal`, `untrailed`). Source: `bolt-teams` spec; ADR 0014 (D2, D5, D7); sluice record 0002; units-build-at-once-and-are-reviewed-in-batches; `plan.py` `run_check`, `merge_command`; `gather.py` `sluice`; `tests/t-sluice.sh`. A fix that carries the bolt onto main becomes its head by crew's move, never a merge: ADR 0015 as restated 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10; `plan.py` `fix_run`, `fix_cmd` and `carry`; `tests/py/test_fix.py`, `tests/t-journey-fix.sh`, `tests/t-journey-land.sh`.

**[teams.12]** Rules out: Touches as prose compared by eye; two units built one after the other because their Touches overlap; a forecast that moves a ref; the same forecast told twice for the same heads; a forecast lost because no agent of the unit was running; the earlier unit told; a forecast against the bolt carried into a unit's prompt beside a newer one against the bolt, which once told a dependent both to rebase onto the bolt alone and to rebase from the upstream crew's rebase used (`tests/t-journey-merge.sh`, 2026-10-12): it is dropped once the newer one is made, however that one reached the unit (`plan.py` `waiting_for`); a dependent forecast against a unit it comes after on that unit's own work its place took, rewritten since by the unit's rebase onto the bolt or its settle, which git, from the bolt both started on, sees both sides change (the units journey before the split, now `tests/t-journey-merge.sh`'s ground). Not ruled out: a dependent's own commit that overlaps the rewritten work, still forecast. Source: ADR 0014 (D3, D8); `units-build-at-once-and-are-reviewed-in-batches` design; `gather.py` `touches`; `plan.py` `overlaps`, `undeclared`, `reach`, `forecasts_due`, `deliver`; `tests/py/test_touches.py`, `tests/py/test_forecast.py`, `tests/contract/test_git.py`; a sibling's work taken by a merge read as the unit's own, 22 false findings on one verify card (crw-2, 2026-10-11, `a-card-lost-to-its-owner-is-posted-again`), ruled out by reading only the unit's first-parent commits; the dependent's false forecast, ruled out by forecasting it against a unit it comes after from the head of that unit its construct's place took (`Took` on the start, `plan.py` `took_heads`), with `git merge-tree --merge-base` (`merge_tree`), which needs git 2.40: every host runs 2.54, swancloud's nixpkgs 26.05 pins for Linux and darwin and mac-studio's own (read 2026-10-12); a start recorded before `Took` existed is compared from the merge base as before.

**[teams.13]** Rules out: a conflict resolved without the other unit's change in hand; the earlier unit rewritten for a later one's conflict; a dependent resolving the conflict its rebase met by a rebase onto the bolt alone, which replays the work crew's rebase left out (`plan.13`). Source: ADR 0014 (D4); the sluice statement on attributing a conflict to the later entry; `plan.py` `forecast_words`, `rebase_words`; `coder.md`.

**[teams.14]** Rules out: a forecast typed into the idle agent of a stage that has already delivered; a settle stage that ends when its pane goes quiet. Source: ADR 0014 (D3); `teams.2`, `teams.4`; `units-build-at-once-and-are-reviewed-in-batches` design; `plan.py` `deliver`, `reach_way`, `deliverable`; `tests/py/test_forecast.py`, `tests/py/test_stage_ends.py`, `tests/t-journey-slots.sh`, `tests/t-journey-merge.sh`, `tests/t-journey-prompts.sh`.

**[teams.15]** Rules out: `herdr agent prompt` with no reading that the prompt landed; a stage reported stuck when it never began. Read on 2026-10-10: `run_stage` in `plugin/bin/crew` sends the prompt with `h agent prompt` and reads nothing back, so crw-2-unit-1's code prompt sat typed and unsubmitted until the operator pressed Enter on the user's word (signal 2026-10-10-crw-2-conductor-fc928fa0/01); `greet` already waits on `blocked` and `a-tell-never-lands-in-a-question` reads the pane's state before a tell, which is the same reading for a different sending. Source: `teams.4`, ADR 0007; `a-stages-prompt-is-taken-or-sent-again`; `plan.py` `run_stage`, `plugin/bin/crew` `prove`; `crew.py` `send_taken` (through a `Pane`) and `tell --taken`; `transcript.py` `pending`, `received` and `end`; `tests/py/test_prompt_taken.py`, `tests/t-journey-slots.sh`, `tests/t-journey-fix.sh`. Read on 2026-10-11, the first construct after the unit landed (crw-1, bolt curation-and-holds, signal 2026-10-11-crw-1-conductor-4687b2ba/01-construct-taken-but-refused): `run_stage` could not read the fresh session's transcript within sixty seconds, under a load of 386, and took the stage as never started, recording the start refused and owing no end, while herdr read the agent as working; forty minutes on the conductor found no transcript of the session at all, so Claude had never reached its first prompt and the pane held no work, the engine still starting under that load (read from the pane: Claude Code at "running SessionStart hooks… 3/4 · 5m 39s" with the prompt submitted, signal 2026-10-11-crw-1-conductor-6e64b9a3/01; the hooks are the fleet's, swancloud `agents.8`); the slot reads as working all the same, so a run again is refused without `--force`, and `crew unit wait` tracks nothing, since it waits only on an owed stage. The rule's "never started" is for a prompt read as not taken twice; a start crew cannot read is `unknown` (`read.1`), watched until the transcript tells, and a working agent with its prompt taken is a stage that began, owed and waited on, whatever the clock said: `a-stage-start-crew-cannot-tell-is-unknown`, `_open.md`.
