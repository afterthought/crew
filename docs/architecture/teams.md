# Team rules

The rules for teams, slots, stages, panes and tells: the bash command, the role launcher and the builder. `core.md` binds here too; the how-to is README's Teams section and `plugin/skills/crew/SKILL.md`. When a stage ends is record 0007.

## Rules

- **[teams.1]** A slot is freed, and its place removed, once its unit, chore or fix has merged, landed, been dropped or left the team's bolt, and its agent has stopped; a team never has more than `units` in flight.
- **[teams.2]** Every unit stage starts as a fresh agent in its slot after the slot's previous agent is ended; crew never starts a role over an agent still running, nor in a pane herdr lists but holds no terminal for, which whatever opens a role's pane, a team's `crew up`, `resume`, `restart` and stage starts, `crew main up`, which starts the partition's operator with the main level (record 0035), a dispatcher's up, `crew console up`, which opens the flow, rail and board tabs and starts no agent, and `crew revive`, closes and opens again in its layout, saying so, `crew revive` then picking up the role's agent there, never touching a pane an agent holds; `stop` fails rather than lies when the agent did not exit, and a working agent is ended, cleared or freed only with `--force`.
- **[teams.3]** A fix is built on `fix/<bolt>/<name>` at `<kit>/places/fix-<bolt>--<name>` from its team's bolt; a fix whose branch or place already exists and that no slot of its team holds is refused, and a fix's agent starts or merges only in a worktree on its own branch tracking the bolt; a fix's agent starts once its place is made, whether or not the place could be made ready, told the step that failed and its words, since a fix is how a bolt is repaired and a bolt whose toolchain is broken takes main by one; and a fix ends by its merge into the bolt, by its conductor's `crew fix <team> <name> --drop`, or with its bolt, its slot freed, its agent ended and its place and branch going as a dropped unit's do.
- **[teams.4]** A stage ends when its deliverable exists in the kit or on the team's host, or when its agent stops short and says what it needs, never when its pane goes quiet; whoever waits learns the end with the deliverable, and a stage quiet past crew's limit is reported stuck.
- **[teams.5]** Code is refused until a unit's change is committed, never for want of a review; verify until every task is ticked, or for a chore until its place holds a commit; merge until every unit or chore it comes after has merged, and a chore's until its verify has delivered, once, no fix round after it calling for another, and its every commit carries its trailer; and a unit, chore or fix merges into its bolt only through `wt submit bolt/<bolt>`, sluice's verdict its gate, in a kit whose `.config/sluice.toml` names its bolt branches' checks; crew gives no bolt in a kit without that policy, naming the file; nothing of crew's moves a bolt's branch, and a bolt that conflicts with main takes main by a submission to the bolt, never by a fix that replays it.
- **[teams.6]** A role's model and effort come only from its definition's frontmatter, overridden only under `roles.<role>` in the teams file, where a partition's main-level role other than its dispatchers may also name the session of the main level's machine it runs in (record 0035); `crew-role` starts every role with `CREW_AGENT`, `CREW_LABEL`, `SLUICE_AGENT` (the agent's name again, which sluice reads) and `CREW_ROLE` (the definition its brief comes from) set and the filled brief appended to the system prompt, in the role's folder, and `crew resume` picks up the agent's last session in the folder it began in; a session herdr resumes without that environment gets its name and identity back at its start, and at each compaction its brief, built by `crew.brief` as `crew-role` builds it, as context.
- **[teams.7]** Every agent crew starts gets a first message once it is up, exactly one per start, so it has a conversation a resume after a herdr restart can pick up: a unit slot its stage's prompt, the reviewer its batch, and every other agent a greeting marked as crew's, a conductor and every agent outside a team (a main level's design agent, planner and ops, a dispatcher, an operator agent) told to carry on with what is theirs, also when `crew revive` brings it back with its conversation, and a team's ops, whenever it begins a new conversation, told who it is, what its team holds and to wait to be told; a plan write whose own command greets a team does not also notify its conductor.
- **[teams.12]** A unit's Touches is a fenced list of paths or globs under `## Touches` in its proposal, read by crew before code; an overlap with an unmerged unit ahead of it in the bolt, and a conflict `git merge-tree` forecasts on the conductor's waits, reach the later unit once, where ahead means earlier in the bolt's merge order, `After` first and the file's order only among units neither comes after, so a unit's dependents are never ahead of it and no overlap note or forecast asks a unit to take the work of one that comes after it; told to its agent when idle, in its next stage's prompt when none runs, or as a settle stage when it waits to merge; a forecast between two unmerged units gives the later `After` on the earlier; overlap orders only merging, never construct, code or verify; a path the unit's own commits changed and it did not declare is a verify finding, never a path of another unit's work its place took.
- **[teams.13]** A conflict between two units, forecast, met at a rebase or met at merge, is resolved in the later unit's place by its agent, handed the other unit's change and the conflicting paths, keeping both intents; the earlier unit is not changed for it.
- **[teams.14]** A settle stage starts, fresh, in the slot of a unit that has delivered its verify, and its fix round when one ran, and waits to merge, when a forecast or an amended `After` unit reaches it; its deliverable is a commit on the unit's branch made since it began, or its agent stopping short to say nothing needs to change, and the unit then merges.
- **[teams.15]** A stage's prompt, and a greeting's, is taken or sent again: crew sends it only once the pane's agent accepts input, reads that the agent's transcript has it within a short time, sends it once more when it has not, and reports the stage as never started when the second sending is not taken either; text left typed in a pane for a hand to submit is never a stage's state.
- **[teams.16]** A team builds one bolt at a time and holds at most one more that is proven and waiting to land, every unit merged and its proof green: from that moment the dispatcher may give the team its next bolt, and crew refuses a give only while the held bolt has an unmerged unit or a proof not yet green; the proven bolt keeps of the team only what its landing needs, a carrying fix's slot and its ops, and the landing still runs on the user's yes.
- **[teams.17]** A unit's or a chore's verify is one round, with no card and no wait on the user: it checks the code against the unit's change as written, and its report marks each finding fix or leave; when it marks one fix, the conductor runs code once more, a fresh coder told the report's path, which fixes every finding marked fix and only those, and the unit then merges with no verify again; a finding marked leave is left; nothing from verify goes to the planner; a fix round that stops short or fails is a stuck stage, run again or passed over only on the user's word; and a unit's merge stage archives its change first, `openspec archive <unit>` committed on its branch, its merge delivered once the bolt holds the change archived, which is the one way a unit has merged: one whose change reached the bolt open inside another unit's merge keeps its place and its own merge owed, named carried in the team's status, and a merge is refused while its branch holds open the change of another unit of the plan that the bolt does not hold archived, naming it.
- **[teams.18]** A unit stage's integration proof is sluice's run: the stage submits the unit's branch for a run that tests it against its bolt and lands nothing, reads the outcome, and starts no seed of its own in its place; every heavy run on a machine is sluice's to start or to lease, and crew holds no count or cap of its own on them; a seed that is no check is sluice's heavy slot and nothing of crew's.
- **[teams.11]** A tell reaches its agent as a prompt or not at all: an agent that is not up, or waiting on a question in its pane, is reported and the command fails with nothing typed.
- **[teams.8]** A pane crew starts answers only Claude's folder-trust question, never the Bypass Permissions warning.
- **[teams.9]** The panes, layout and slots files change only by writing a new file and moving it over the old; the stages file changes only under `flock`; output that bash evals is shell assignments quoted with `shlex.quote`, and everything else such a command says goes to standard error.
- **[teams.10]** A brief fills every `{{TOKEN}}` from the builder, a token with no builder or no data is a refusal, never blank text, and every brief carries the roster with how to read crew's marks.
