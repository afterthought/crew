# Plan rules

The rules for the plan, proposals, stages, worktrees and every write to a flywheel's branch. `core.md` binds here too; the how-to is README's The plan, Proposals and State sections. Why a drop keeps unmerged work is record 0003; why a change is archived at merge is 0006.

## Rules

- **[plan.1]** Every write runs `recfix --check` on each `.rec` file it changed and crew's own rules (a unit's bolt in the same repo, `After` within one bolt, no cycle) before committing, and its change function re-checks every precondition against the tip it is applied to, since it replays.
- **[plan.2]** Every commit on a flywheel's branch has a subject ending ` (<agent>)` and a `Crew-Entry:` trailer naming its run-record entry, and carries the host's uncarried run record; a carry-only commit names no entry.
- **[plan.3]** A proposal holds only the plan commands a proposal may hold, parsed by the command line's own grammar and applied by the same functions as a direct run; it is checked by applying each `Do` in order to a copy at the tip, one refusal refuses the whole, and approval applies every `Do` the user read and kept in order at the tip and closes the proposal in one commit, or changes nothing; a unit new to the bolt the user cleared on the card is left out with every command that only concerns it, a kept command that names it refuses the whole, and the approval records what was cleared.
- **[plan.4]** A proposal is numbered once, never removed, its `Do` lines never changed; it closes only as approved or dropped, and one that touches a held bolt is approved only after that bolt's conductor has agreed.
- **[plan.5]** Work a held bolt needs before it can be proven or land is placed in that bolt ahead of its first unmerged unit, marked `--unblocks`; a proposal that marks a unit so and places it elsewhere is refused.
- **[plan.6]** A unit that has merged or landed is never amended, split, moved or dropped; a unit marked `Amended` reads amended, then construct, and its code, verify and merge are refused until its construct has committed again, with no approval asked.
- **[plan.7]** A unit's name is its OpenSpec change's name, refused when the kit's main already holds a change by that name, open or archived.
- **[plan.8]** A bolt is cut from the kit's main as it stands at that moment, meets main only at its landing, which is main-level ops's submission of the bolt's branch to main through sluice, landed as a merge commit carrying sluice's verdict once main with the bolt passes main's checks, and a landing is refused while the kit's main on its host is behind GitHub's; when the bolt and main conflict nothing lands, the conflict is the bolt's, and the team takes main into the bolt by a submission to the bolt and submits again; no agent, and nothing of crew's, moves a bolt's branch or main by git.
- **[plan.9]** crew removes only worktrees it made, directly under `<kit>/places/` on a `unit/`, `chore/` or `fix/` branch, and a bolt's worktree made before record 0026 under `<kit>/bolts/` on its `bolt/` branch, once their work is finished, and removes each through the kit's own worktree tool so the kit's remove hooks run and what the kit stood up for that worktree goes with it, a hook that fails keeping the worktree, named; a worktree with modified or non-ignored untracked files, a locked one, the main checkout and a hand-made one are never removed, and nothing is removed while a plan the kit's work goes in cannot be read.
- **[plan.10]** A branch holding commits whose patch the bolt or main lacks, judged by `git cherry`, is kept and named when its worktree goes; a branch holding nothing beyond its target is removed.
- **[plan.11]** A chore is planned maintenance built inside a bolt by code, verify and merge, with no construct and no review, and changes nothing a spec says must be true; it is a Unit record of the plan marked `Kind: chore`, its stage read from its trailer on main or the bolt and its place, and `After` holds for it as for any unit (`plan.13`), its code being what a dependent builds on.
- **[plan.12]** A command reads each kit once and fetches the state branch once, however many steps it has, and stage facts read without `openspec` agree with openspec's on the same worktree.
- **[plan.13]** `After` names a unit whose code the dependent builds on, a contour that exists only once that unit's code exists, or a unit a forecast conflict between the two put it after; the dependent's construct starts only once each has finished construct, never waiting on a merge, and the dependent merges only after each has merged into the bolt; `After` is never set by hand for shared files or for product order, which is the file's.
- **[plan.14]** A landing records the sha main holds afterwards and deploys nothing; a deploy is one act by the kit's deployer under the environment's policy, to one environment, over every landing since that environment's last deployed state, recorded in the run record as it runs with its contents, the kit's after-deploy checks and their outcomes, and every after-deploy step of the landings it carries with its doer and state; a `manual` environment's deploy is a rail row with a card held to approve, a step that is the user's, or an agent's held for the user's word, is a rail row with a card until the user marks it done or skipped or says when the agent does it, and promotion to the next environment is a deploy to it.
- **[plan.15]** An amendment the user approves runs the unit's construct again by that approval's own act, on the team's host with the amendment's words, ending the unit's agent where it stands when it is past construct, and withdraws the unit's stale card through its owner's notice; a conductor brought back finds an amended unit with no construct running and starts it; nothing waits on anyone remembering; and the approval's command answers once its write is made and the construct's start is handed to the team's host, which reads the start and tells the conductor what started or why nothing did, so no plan write waits on an agent's session coming up or its prompt landing, and whoever needs the construct waits with `crew unit wait`.
- **[plan.16]** A bolt lands by one procedure, main-level ops's: the user's approval of the bolt's landing card, which crew raises in its conductor's name once the bolt's verification is green at its head (record 0025) and the conductor passes on with `crew tell`, or the user's words in ops's pane, is the word to land and to push; ops submits the bolt's branch to main through sluice, which lands it as a merge commit or refuses it with the conflict named for the team, pushes main without force, runs `crew bolt land`, and for crew brings every host's checkout up to main; a bolt no team holds, built outside a team, whose every unit's change and chore its kit's main already holds, lands by `crew bolt land` alone on the user's word, nothing merged or pushed, recording the commit main holds as any landing does; nothing else is a word to land, and a landing deploys nothing.
- **[plan.17]** crew makes a worktree ready only through its kit's declaration in `.config/sluice.toml`, by the declared toolchain and then the declared runner's setup, run in the worktree on the team's host with sluice's cleaned environment, whenever it makes the worktree or finds it made; a kit that declares nothing has nothing run; crew enforces no floor on the version of a kit's runner and refuses a moon only when it cannot read its version, sluice's moon runner keeping its own floor, moon 2.6, at its run; crew names no kit script, reads no kit's profile, and assumes neither moon nor devenv.

## Details

**[plan.1]** Rules out: a write that skips `recfix`; a precondition checked once before the replay loop. Source: `plan.py` 263–328, 390–406, 488–500. `land` checks every file a commit holds, the carried `runs/<host>/` files among them; a signal's or capture's markdown is checked by its frontmatter, read as one record.

**[plan.2]** Rules out: a commit with no trailer; two acts in one commit with one entry. Source: `flywheel-state` spec "A commit names its run-record entry"; `plan.py` 449–535.

**[plan.3]** Rules out: a proposal as prose; `bolt give` in a `Do`; a half-applied approval; a cleared unit's `--after` left standing. Not ruled out: the approval of a subset the user chose on the card, unit by unit (ADR 0018). Not built: `_open.md`. Source: `plan-proposals` spec; design "Each plan command becomes three parts"; `plan.py` 1122–1165, 1797–1845.

**[plan.4]** Rules out: editing `proposals.rec`; reusing a number; approving over a missing agreement. Source: `plan-proposals` spec; `plan.py` 1498–1510, 1710–1774.

**[plan.5]** Rules out: an `--unblocks` unit in the queue. Source: fix 902336e; `plan-proposals` spec "Work a bolt in flight needs to land goes into that bolt".

**[plan.6]** Rules out: `unit split` on a unit in code; construct on a merged unit; an amended unit held for the user's approval. Source: `bolt-plan` spec; unit-amendments design; ADR 0014 (D5); units-build-at-once-and-are-reviewed-in-batches; `plan.py` `place_stage`, `run_check`, `unmark`; `tests/py/test_stages.py`, `tests/py/test_stage_rules.py`, `tests/py/test_amend.py`.

**[plan.7]** Rules out: a unit named after an archived change. Source: `plan.py` 808–817.

**[plan.8]** Rules out: a landing onto a main behind GitHub, which could not be pushed without force; `git reset`, `git branch -f`, a merge, a rebase or a fast-forward of a bolt's branch or of main by an agent or by crew, which sluice's guard refuses once its hook is installed in the kit (sluice `crates/sluice/src/protect.rs`, read 2026-10-13), among them crew's fast-forward of the kit's main to GitHub's at a give; a fix that replays the bolt's commits onto main and crew's move of the bolt's branch to it, with the judgment of such a fix by `git cherry` or by its commits' messages and authors (ADR 0015, superseded by record 0026: crw-2, 2026-10-11, main's crew paired the bolt's two identical empty approval commits out of order). Not ruled out: a fix whose agent merges main into its place and is submitted to the bolt, which is how the team takes main into it. A landing is a submission from a worktree on the bolt's branch, the one place sluice takes a submission from (sluice `crates/sluice/src/profiles/worktrunk/mod.rs`, which refuses a detached head), made by crew on the team's host and removed after; sluice moves main's branch by `update-ref` and not the main checkout's files, so crew brings that checkout level with `git read-tree -m -u`, as sluice's own landings are followed (read 2026-10-13); the same holds for a bolt given with a worktree before record 0026 (`<kit>/bolts/<bolt>`, kept until it lands, point 5): sluice moves `bolt/<bolt>` at each merge and leaves that worktree at the old head, which refuses the next submission, so crew's merge step brings it level with `git read-tree -m -u <old> bolt/<bolt>` in it, saying when it could not, as `LANDING` does the main checkout; a bolt given since has no worktree and nothing to level (wldn's atl-1 on chuck-herdr-alpha, 2026-10-14, every merge refused until ops reset the worktree by hand; signal 2026-10-14-swancloud-planner-afbbdc00/01; `a-bolts-worktree-is-levelled-after-a-merge`, not built: `_open.md`); a `git reset --hard` of the kit's main checkout or of a bolt's worktree is ruled out too, by an agent or by crew, though it moves no ref, since it discards whatever the checkout holds uncommitted, where `read-tree -m -u` refuses rather than overwrites (`plan.10`: keeping work by mistake is the safer error), and a crew older than the leveling step leaves the checkout at the old tree, its landing showing as staged reversals, until a hand brings it level the same way (wldn-ops on chuck-herdr-alpha, 2026-10-14, whose crew is held at box-hold 2c1774c: aws-bootstrap's checkout after landings #1 and #2 reset by hand after reading the tree equal to the old commit and nothing untracked, atlas-kit's after #5 and #6 left, holding an unstaged edit and three untracked files; signal 2026-10-14-swancloud-planner-4a9e7330/01). Source: fix 04daa5d; record 0026; `plan.py` `BOLT_BRANCH`, `bolt_give`, `MAIN`, `main_held`, `LANDING`, `landing_said`, `bolt_submit`; `tests/py/test_landing.py`, `tests/contract/test_git.py`, `tests/t-journey-teams.sh`, `tests/t-journey-land.sh`.

**[plan.9]** A chore's place is `<kit>/places/chore-<name>` on `chore/<name>` (`plan.11`). A fix's work is over when it has merged into its bolt, when its conductor dropped it (`teams.3`, record 0028), or when no plan has its bolt; a unit's once its change is archived on its bolt or main, never while the bolt holds it open (`teams.17`). Rules out: tidying `main`; removing a dirty worktree; removing anything when a plan is unreadable; `git worktree remove` on a kit whose `.config/wt.toml` has a `[pre-remove]` or `[post-remove]` hook (swb-3-ops, 2026-10-09: switchboard-kit's hook tears down the worktree's ministack and its Frontegg Development objects, and three fix worktrees' stood after crew removed them, signal 2026-10-09-swb-3-ops-401c8aa3/01 on wldn/main). Read the same day: `wt remove` runs pre-remove hooks with the worktree's files still there, post-remove after, and deletes the branch only when merged, with `--no-delete-branch` to leave it to `plan.10`'s judgment; crew's and swancloud's kits have merge hooks only. A bolt's branch crew made, with no worktree since record 0026, is marked `branch.bolt/<bolt>.crew` in the kit's git config, which git drops with the branch, and goes once no plan has the bolt and no worktree is on it; a deletion sluice's guard refuses while a submission to the branch waits is kept and named. Source: worktrees-go-with-their-work; records 0026 and 0028; `plan.py` `tidy`, `tidy_over`, `kept_why`, `wt_refused`, `BOLT_BRANCH`, `bolt_branch_over`, `fixes_dropped`, `fix_branch_gone`; `gather.py` `archived`; `tests/py/test_tidy.py`, `tests/contract/test_wt.py`, `tests/contract/test_git.py`, `tests/t-journey-land.sh`, `tests/t-journey-carried.sh`.

**[plan.10]** Rules out: `git branch -D` on a branch with unmerged patches; judging by ancestry across a rebase. Source: ADR 0003; a dropped fix's branch, record 0028, `plan.py` `fix_branch_kept`, `fix_branch_gone`. A dropped fix's branch is judged by `git cherry` against its bolt, and kept and named when it tracks no bolt or git cannot compare them; a dropped fix whose place merged main holds main's commits beyond the bolt, so its branch is kept and named though the fix wrote nothing of its own, since keeping work by mistake is the safer error (the recovery session, 2026-10-13; `a-fix-is-dropped` design). Every other branch is deleted outright, and judged by ancestry, until `a-drop-keeps-unmerged-work` lands: `_open.md`.

**[plan.11]** Rules out: a construct stage for a chore; a chore that writes a spec; a chore in a review batch; a chore's stage kept by hand; a third record set, whose records recutils keeps together after their descriptor, so the file's order could not be the build order of units and chores together; a dependent of a chore waiting on the chore's merge, which `plan.13` rules out for any work: a dependent of a chore starts once the chore's code has delivered, from the chore's head, as its start's `Took` records (the user, 2026-10-12: a chore should not differ from any other unit). Source: ADR 0001 as amended 2026-10-12, on the user's ruling: a Unit record marked `Kind: chore`, in build order, since recutils keeps a record set's records together after their descriptor; `chores-beside-units-and-fixes`. Built by `plan.py` `is_chore`, `Plan.work`, the unit ops with the kind from the command (`kind_refusal`), `Stages` (`chore_stage`, `after_met`, `built_on`), `took_from` and `TAKE`, `chore_check` (`chore_refusal`, `untrailed`, `CHORE_LOG`), `rebase_dependents`, `Owed` and `delivered`, `slot_stage`, `tidy_over`, and `gather.py` `chores`, `chore_place` and `own_head`; proved by `tests/py/test_chores.py`, `tests/contract/test_gather.py`, `tests/contract/test_recfix.py` and `tests/t-journey-chores.sh`.

**[plan.12]** Rules out: a second `gather` in one command; a stage fact that disagrees with openspec. The direct reading knows openspec's own spec-driven schema; a change of any other schema, of a spec-driven its kit or user redefines, or with metadata crew does not read, is read through `openspec`. Source: `openspec/explorations/command-cost/reading.md`; ADR 0008; a-command-reads-the-kits-once; `plan.py` `Reading`, in the memory of the one process every crew command is, `fetch`, `land`, `survey`; `gather.py` `tasks_of`, `planned`; `tests/t-stage-facts.sh`; `tests/contract/test_gather.py`; `tests/t-journey-units.sh` and `tests/t-journey-slots.sh`, whose status and unit run each fetch and gather once, and `tests/t-journey-board.sh`, whose rail tab's refresh does.

**[plan.13]** Rules out: `After` set by hand between units that only touch the same files; a dependent's construct before its dependency's change is committed; a dependent's construct held for its dependency's merge; a dependent merged before its dependency; `After` as sequencing; a dependent forecast to conflict with its dependency on the dependency's own work its place took, once the dependency's rebase or settle rewrote it: the dependent is forecast against it from the head its place took (`teams.12`); a dependent's rebase at its dependency's merge that replays the dependency's commits as its place took them, and so conflicts on the dependency's own files once its settle rewrote them: the rebase leaves out each merged After unit's head the dependent took and still holds, from an upstream with no ref whose parents are the bolt and those heads, since `git rebase --onto` the bolt from the taken head alone replays the bolt's commits made between the two places and conflicts where the bolt changed them again (both read 2026-10-12 in `tests/contract/test_git.py`); a dependent whose rebase conflicts told to rebase onto the bolt alone, which replays the work crew's rebase left out: it is told the command that makes that upstream again, from the bolt as it then stands and the heads its place holds (`rebase_words`, the heads on the forecast's `Took`), since the upstream has no ref and nothing keeps it; crew writes no ref for it, so there is nothing to remove after and nothing for `read.4` or `plan.9` to allow. What these leave, an After unit's work taken by hand after the place took its head and the other After units' work in one forecast: `_open.md`. Not ruled out: the `After` a conductor sets on a forecast conflict between two unmerged units (`teams.12`). A chore holds to this rule as a unit does, its code being what a dependent builds on (`plan.11`). Source: ADR 0011; ADR 0014 (D1 to D3); `bolt-plan` spec "The file's order and dependencies are the plan"; units-build-at-once-and-are-reviewed-in-batches; `plan.py` `Stages`, `run_check`, `take`, `rebase_dependents`, `rebase_leaves_out`, `ONTO`, `rebase_words`, `amended_afters`, `took_heads`, `forecasts_due`; `tests/py/test_stage_rules.py`, `tests/py/test_forecast.py`, `tests/contract/test_git.py`, `tests/t-journey-slots.sh`, `tests/t-journey-merge.sh`.
